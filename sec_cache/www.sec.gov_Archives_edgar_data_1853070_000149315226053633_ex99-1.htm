SOAR AI Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results with $41.2 Million in Cash and $1.2 Billion Under Contract; Revenue Up 245%

 

Aviation AI and software segment delivers positive operating income; Ohio campus advancing toward service of contracted AI capacity backed by approximately $215 million revenue backstop

 

NORTH CANTON, Ohio — November 23, 2026 — SOAR AI, Inc. (NYSE American: SOAR) (“SOAR AI” or the “Company”) today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, its first completed quarter following the addition of its AI infrastructure business through the September 11, 2026 merger with Alignment Engine Inc.

 

Third Quarter Financial Highlights

 

●Cash of $41.2 million, compared to $4.7 million at year-end 2025
●Revenue up 245% to $1.3 million, compared to $0.4 million in prior year; gross profit of $0.9 million, compared to $0.3 million in prior year
●Vaunt annual recurring revenue (“ARR”) of approximately $5.9 million, compared to $1.8 million in prior year
●Aviation AI and software segment operating income of $0.2 million, compared to a loss of $0.6 million in prior year
●Adjusted net loss of $2.1 million, or $0.04 per share; net loss of $33.2 million, or $0.62 per share
●Adjusted EBITDA loss of $1.6 million, compared to $2.5 million in prior year
●Shareholders’ equity of $87.5 million, which the Company expects will allow it to regain NYSE American compliance in December

 

Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted net loss are non-GAAP financial measures, and ARR is an operating metric; see “Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures” and the reconciliations below.

 

NYSE American Continued Listing

 

As of September 30, 2026, shareholders’ equity was $87.5 million, compared with a shareholders’ deficit of $1.8 million at December 31, 2025. The Company believes this satisfies NYSE American’s continued listing standards for stockholders’ equity under Section 1003(a) of the NYSE American Company Guide, pending formal exchange confirmation, which the Company expects to receive in December 2026.

 

Business Highlights

 

●AI infrastructure added to the Company’s portfolio: Completed the merger with Alignment Engine, Inc. on September 11, 2026, adding an AI infrastructure business alongside the Company’s aviation software business. The Company’s Board of Directors and public company management team remained in place following the merger, other than the transition of the CEO position to Chris Ensey and his addition to the board, and the Company completed its name change to SOAR AI, Inc. on October 19, 2026.

 

 

 

 

●$1.17 billion under contract: Signed a master services agreement and two four-year, take-or-pay orders with a large AI customer representing $1.17 billion in aggregate contract value and providing for approximately $133 million in customer prepayments.
●Revenue backstop on the initial deployment: A three-year revenue backstop of approximately $215 million supports the initial deployment of 4,096 AMD Instinct™ MI355X GPUs, providing a floor under revenue from that deployment alongside the customer’s take-or-pay commitment, including coverage of capacity not used under the customer orders.
●2027 revenue outlook reiterated: Based on contractual start dates, the Company continues to expect the signed orders to generate revenue of $175 million to $200 million in 2027 and approximately $294 million of annual revenue once both orders are in full service.
●Vaunt ARR growth: Vaunt ARR increased every month in 2026, reaching approximately $5.9 million as of September 30, 2026, up 139% from December 31, 2025.
●Proprietary technology: Granted U.S. Patent No. 12,775,572 covering technology underlying NeuralSync™, the Company’s AI infrastructure technology platform.

 

Ohio Campus Build Update

 

●Construction management: In October, the Company engaged a construction manager for the Ohio AI infrastructure campus under a cost-plus agreement with guaranteed maximum prices set for each phase.
●Equipment on order: GPU server and rack orders for the initial deployment are in place, along with long-lead electrical and data center equipment, and key equipment has begun arriving on site.
●Data hall readiness: The data hall floor and ESD coating are complete and plate heat exchangers are on site.
●Timeline: The campus remains on track for internal power-on by December 31, 2026.
●Power: The campus has 154 MW of power available today and a roadmap to 480 MW.

 

“The capacity for our first deployment in Ohio is under four-year take-or-pay orders, and we expect to power on the campus in the coming weeks. We are building SOAR AI for customers who want dedicated AI capacity under their own control,” said Chris Ensey, Chief Executive Officer of SOAR AI.

 

“Our reported results this quarter reflect accounting for the merger, most significantly non-cash charges for the note we issued as consideration for certain waivers in connection with our convertible note facility, a step that was deemed necessary by the parties to complete the merger, and for the remeasurement of the warrants issued in the merger,” said Mark Heinen, Chief Financial Officer of SOAR AI. “Excluding those items, our adjusted EBITDA loss narrowed from a year ago, and our Aviation AI and software segment reached positive operating income. With take-or-pay orders, customer prepayments and a revenue backstop on our first deployment, our focus now is on completing the financing for our contracted deployments and delivering our first capacity on schedule.”

 

 

 

 

Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted net loss from continuing operations, the non-GAAP measures presented in this press release and supplementary information, are not a measure of performance under the accounting principles generally accepted in the United States (“GAAP”). These non-GAAP financial measures have limitations as an analytical tool, including that they do not have standardized meanings. Management uses these measures to evaluate period-to-period comparisons of operating performance and believes they provide meaningful supplemental information by excluding items that may not be indicative of the Company’s ongoing operating results. Management believes that both management and investors benefit from referring to these measures in assessing the Company’s performance. When assessing our operating performance, these non-GAAP financial measures should not be considered, and investors should also consider, net loss, net loss from continuing operations, loss per share and other measures of performance as defined by GAAP as indicators of the Company’s performance or profitability.

 

Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 included $31.2 million of non-cash and merger-related items, primarily a $25.1 million non-cash charge for the convertible note that was issued as consideration for certain waivers in connection with the Company’s convertible note facility, which was deemed necessary by the parties to complete the merger, a $4.7 million non-cash loss on the change in fair value of the replacement warrants issued in the merger, as well as merger-related costs and amortization of acquired intangible assets. Excluding these items and other adjustments described below, the Adjusted EBITDA loss improved to $1.6 million from $2.5 million in the third quarter of 2025, and the adjusted net loss from continuing operations was $2.1 million, or $0.04 per share. Deferred revenue and customer deposits were $45.7 million as of September 30, 2026, primarily customer prepayments that will be recognized as revenue as service is delivered.

 

This press release includes EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA and adjusted net loss from continuing operations, which are non-GAAP financial measures. The Company defines EBITDA as net income (loss) before interest income (expense), net, provision (benefit) for income taxes and depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA is EBITDA further adjusted to exclude net income (loss) from discontinued operations, gains and losses from changes in the fair value of financial instruments, other income (expense), net, stock-based compensation, merger-related costs and non-recurring legal costs. Merger-related costs consist of legal, fairness opinion, purchase price allocation and other professional fees and costs incurred in connection with the merger. Non-recurring legal costs consist of legal fees for the Company’s merger-related proxy statement, registration statement and financing transactions in the period following the merger. Adjusted net loss from continuing operations is net loss from continuing operations excluding gains and losses from changes in the fair value of financial instruments, merger-related costs, non-recurring legal costs, amortization of acquired intangible assets and stock-based compensation for replacement options issued in the merger, and the related income tax effects.

 

 

 

 

The Company believes these measures help investors evaluate its operating performance by excluding items related to the merger and other non-cash items that do not reflect the underlying performance of its operations. The convertible note was issued for no cash proceeds as consideration for certain waivers in connection with the Company’s convertible note facility, which was deemed necessary by the parties to complete the merger, and the related charge is not expected to recur. These measures have limitations, are not presented in accordance with GAAP, should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for measures prepared in accordance with GAAP, and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies.

 

Annual recurring revenue, or ARR, is an operating metric that the Company calculates as the annualized value of recurring subscription revenue under active Vaunt membership agreements as of the end of the period. ARR is not a measure of revenue under GAAP, should be viewed independently of revenue and deferred revenue, and is not intended to replace or forecast revenue. The Company’s calculation of ARR may differ from similarly titled metrics used by other companies.

About SOAR AI, Inc.

 

SOAR AI, Inc. (NYSE American: SOAR) is an AI infrastructure and software company focused on the development and operation of AI infrastructure and AI-powered software solutions. The Company is constructing an AI infrastructure campus in Ohio that combines data center infrastructure, high-density GPU compute, high-performance networking and related technology to support AI, machine learning and high-performance computing workloads. The name SOAR, which stands for Sovereign AI Resources, reflects the Company’s strategy of developing AI infrastructure that provides customers greater control over their data, models and computing environments.

 

The Company also develops AI-powered operational systems for aviation businesses through its Aviation AI platform and operates Vaunt, a technology-enabled private aviation membership platform.

 

For more information, visit soarai.com.
