SoundThinking, Inc. Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

FREMONT, CA – November 12, 2026 – SoundThinking, Inc. (Nasdaq: SSTI), a leading public safety technology company, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operational Highlights

•
Revenues of $25.0 million remained relatively flat year over year, compared to $25.1 million for the same quarter of 2025.
•
Gross profit decreased 13% to $11.9 million (48% of revenues), compared to $13.6 million (54% of revenues) for the same quarter of 2025.
•
GAAP net loss totaled $8.5 million, compared to GAAP net loss of $2.0 million for the same quarter of 2025.
•
Adjusted EBITDA1 totaled $2.2 million (9% of revenues), compared to $3.5 million (14% of revenues) for the same quarter of 2025.
•
Expanded with three existing customers.

 

1 See the section below titled “Non-GAAP Financial Measures” for more information about Adjusted EBITDA and its reconciliation to GAAP net (loss).

Transaction with Transom

 

As previously announced on September 29, 2026, SoundThinking entered into a definitive merger agreement (the "Merger Agreement") under which certain affiliates of Transom Capital Group, LLC ("Transom") will acquire SoundThinking through a tender offer for $8.00 per share in cash, and one non-transferable contingent value right (“CVR”) worth up to $3.00 per share, for aggregate potential consideration of up to $11.00 per share in cash. The implied enterprise value of SoundThinking based solely on the up-front cash consideration is approximately $114 million and the total enterprise value with payment of the maximum CVR payment is approximately $159 million. SoundThinking expects to close the transaction, which is referred to herein as the “Transom Transaction,” in the fourth quarter of 2026, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions, including SoundThinking’s stockholders validly tendering shares of SoundThinking’s common stock representing at least a majority of the outstanding shares of SoundThinking common stock. Following closing of the Transom Transaction, SoundThinking will become a privately held company.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Revenues for the third quarter of 2026 were $25.0 million, compared to $25.1 million for the same quarter of 2025. The decrease in revenues was primarily attributable to a reduction in revenue of approximately $1.0 million resulting from the non-renewal or delays of renewals of our contracts with multiple customers, partially offset by $0.7 million in new bookings and expansions with existing customers and $0.2 million in catch-up revenue from various customers.

 

Gross profit for the third quarter of 2026 was $11.9 million (48% of revenues), compared to $13.6 million (54% of revenues) for the same period in 2025 reflecting lower revenue volume, including lower catch-up revenue, and an increase in information technology and facility costs.

Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were $20.1 million, compared to $15.7 million for the same period in 2025. The increase was primarily due to increased acquisition-related costs for the Transom Transaction and increased sales and marketing expenses, partially offset by decreased research and development costs related to a decrease in headcount.

Net loss for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $8.5 million or $(0.64) per basic and diluted share (based on 13.2 million basic and diluted weighted-average shares outstanding), compared to net loss of $2.0 million or $(0.16) per basic and diluted share (based on 12.8 million basic and diluted weighted-average shares outstanding) for the same period in 2025.

Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 totaled $2.2 million, compared to $3.5 million in the same period last year.

At quarter end, the company had $9.5 million in cash and cash equivalents, $29.2 million in accounts receivable and contract assets, net, $41.7 million in deferred revenue, $4.0 million in debt and approximately $36.0 million available on its credit facility.

Business Outlook and Earnings Conference Call

 

In light of the pending Transom Transaction, SoundThinking will not be providing forward-looking guidance or updates to previously issued guidance and will not host an earnings conference call or webcast for its third quarter 2026 financial results and does not expect to do so for any future quarters. Earnings materials are publicly available on SoundThinking’s Investor Relations website at https://ir.soundthinking.com/.

 

Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

Adjusted EBITDA: Adjusted EBITDA, a non-GAAP financial measure, represents the company’s net income (loss) before interest (income) expense, income taxes, depreciation, amortization and impairment, restructuring expense, acquisition-related expenses and stock-based compensation expense. Adjusted EBITDA is a measure used by management internally to understand and evaluate the company’s core operating performance and trends across accounting periods and in connection with developing future operating plans, making strategic decisions regarding the allocation of capital and considering initiatives focused on cultivating new markets for its solutions. In particular, the exclusion of these expenses in calculating Adjusted EBITDA facilitates comparisons of the company’s operating performance on a period-to-period basis.

SoundThinking believes Adjusted EBITDA also provides useful information to investors and others in understanding and evaluating its operating results in the same manner as its management and board of directors. For example, SoundThinking adjusts EBITDA for stock-based compensation expense because such expenses often vary for reasons that are generally unrelated to financial and operational performance in a particular period. Stock-based compensation is utilized by SoundThinking to attract and retain employees with a goal of long-term retention and the alignment of employee interests with those of the company and its stockholders, rather than to address operational performance for any particular period’s financial performance measures, in particular net loss, or its other GAAP financial results.

About SoundThinking, Inc.

 

SoundThinking, Inc. (Nasdaq: SSTI) is a leading public safety technology company that delivers AI- and data-driven solutions for law enforcement, civic leadership, and security professionals. SoundThinking is trusted by more than 300 customers and has worked with approximately 2,100 agencies to drive more efficient, effective, and equitable public safety outcomes. The company’s SafetySmartTM platform includes ShotSpotter®, the leading acoustic gunshot detection system; CrimeTracerTM, the leading law enforcement search engine; CaseBuilderTM, a one-stop investigation management system; ResourceRouterTM, software that directs patrol and community anti-violence resources to help maximize their impact; PlateRangerTM powered by Rekor, a leading ALPR solution; Field AgentTM, an AI layer that transforms public safety data

into actionable intelligence; and SafePointe®, an AI-based weapons detection system. SoundThinking has been designated a Great Place to Work® company.
