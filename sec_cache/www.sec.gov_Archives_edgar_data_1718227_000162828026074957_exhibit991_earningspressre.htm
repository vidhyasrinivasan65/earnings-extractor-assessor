NEWS RELEASE
Construction Partners, Inc. Announces Fiscal 2026
Fourth Quarter and Full Year Results

Revenue Up 31% Compared to FY25
Net Income Up 40% Compared to FY25
Adjusted EBITDA Up 24% Compared to FY25
Company Reiterates Fiscal 2027 Outlook

DOTHAN, AL, November 19, 2026 – Construction Partners, Inc. (NASDAQ: ROAD) (“CPI” or the “Company”), a vertically integrated civil infrastructure company specializing in the construction and maintenance of roadways in local markets throughout the Sunbelt, today announced financial and operating results for its fiscal fourth quarter and year ended September 30, 2026.
Fred J. (Jule) Smith, III, the Company’s President and Chief Executive Officer, stated, “We delivered fourth quarter results in line with the preliminary financial ranges we announced in early November, capping off a year of significant growth for CPI. During fiscal 2026, we advanced our ROAD 2030 goals through organic growth initiatives and seven strategic acquisitions that expanded our presence in high-growth markets, increased our market share, and strengthened vertical integration. Strong infrastructure demand and active commercial construction across the Sunbelt supported 7% organic revenue growth for the year. We continue to build on this organic growth momentum by expanding market share across our recently acquired businesses. Disciplined execution across our operations, powered by nearly 7,700 employees, continues to translate this demand into improved results through safe, efficient project execution.
“Subsequent to the end of fiscal 2026, we completed three additional acquisitions expanding operations in attractive growth markets in Texas, Florida and Georgia. Entering fiscal 2027, we are well-positioned for continued growth, supported by robust Sunbelt economic fundamentals, continued strong public infrastructure investment in our states, and ongoing opportunities for acquisitions. These strong fundamentals support our fiscal 2027 outlook for approximately 15% growth in both revenue and Adjusted EBITDA. We look forward to capturing the opportunities ahead and creating lasting value for all stakeholders.”
Fiscal 2026 Financial Results
Revenue in fiscal 2026 was $3.674 billion, an increase of 31% compared to $2.812 billion in fiscal 2025.
Net income in fiscal 2026 was $142.1 million, an increase of 40% compared to $101.8 million in fiscal 2025.
Adjusted net income(1) in fiscal 2026 was $154.5 million, an increase of 27% compared to $122.0 million in fiscal 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA(1) in fiscal 2026 was $523.5 million, an increase of 24% compared to $423.7 million in fiscal 2025.
Adjusted EBITDA margin(1) in fiscal 2026 was 14.2%, compared to 15.1% in fiscal 2025.
Project backlog was approximately $3.29 billion at September 30, 2026, compared to $3.36 billion at June 30, 2026 and $3.03 billion at September 30, 2025.
Smith continued, “Following record fourth quarter revenue, our backlog entering fiscal 2027 strongly supports our guidance of 15% revenue growth. Additionally, in the first few weeks of fiscal 2027, we completed three acquisitions that added $65.0 million of backlog, substantially all of which we expect to convert to revenue in fiscal 2027. Looking beyond the first half of fiscal 2027, we see two primary catalysts for meaningful margin improvement: the continued integration of our fiscal 2026 acquisitions and improved recovery of elevated diesel fuel costs through project pricing. Ned N. Fleming, III, the Company’s Executive Chairman, stated, “This year once again showed the strength of CPI’s differentiated business model. Our industry remains highly fragmented, and as many family-owned businesses across the Sunbelt navigate generational transitions, CPI has become an acquirer of choice for experienced operators who want to preserve their legacy and grow their businesses. The ten acquisitions we have completed since the start of fiscal 2026 are a testament to how repeatable this approach has become. Looking ahead, the long-term drivers of demand in our markets, including aging infrastructure, population growth and rising traffic volumes, remain firmly intact, and our states and municipalities continue to show a strong commitment to roadway investment. About Construction Partners, Inc.
Construction Partners, Inc. is a vertically integrated civil infrastructure company operating in local markets throughout the Sunbelt in Alabama, Florida, Georgia, North Carolina, Oklahoma, South Carolina, Tennessee and Texas. Supported by its hot-mix asphalt plants, aggregate facilities and liquid asphalt terminals, the Company focuses on the construction, repair and maintenance of surface infrastructure. Publicly funded projects make up the majority of its business and include local and state roadways, interstate highways, airport runways and bridges. The Company also performs private sector projects that include paving and sitework for office and industrial parks, shopping centers, local businesses and residential developments. To learn more, visit www.constructionpartners.net.

Reconciliation of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

Adjusted EBITDA represents net income before, as applicable from time to time, (i) interest expense, net, (ii) provision (benefit) for income taxes, (iii) depreciation, depletion, accretion and amortization, (iv) share-based compensation expense, (v) loss on the extinguishment of debt, and (vi) nonrecurring expenses related to transformative acquisitions, which management considers to include transactions of a size that would require clearance under federal antitrust laws. Adjusted EBITDA margin represents Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of revenues for each period. Adjusted net income represents net income before (i) nonrecurring expenses related to transformative acquisitions, which management considers to include transactions of a size that would require clearance under federal antitrust laws, and (ii) nonrecurring fees associated with financing arrangements incurred in connection with transformative acquisitions. These metrics are supplemental measures of our operating performance that are neither required by, nor presented in accordance with, GAAP. These measures have limitations as analytical tools and should not be considered in isolation or as an alternative to net income or any other performance measure derived in accordance with GAAP as an indicator of our operating performance. We present Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin and Adjusted net income because management uses these measures as key performance indicators, and we believe that securities analysts, investors and others use these measures to evaluate companies in our industry. Our calculation of Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA margin and Adjusted net income may not be comparable to similarly named measures reported by other companies. Potential differences may include differences in capital structures, tax positions and the age and book depreciation of intangible and tangible assets.
