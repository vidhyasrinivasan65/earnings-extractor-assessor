TRUEBLUE REPORTS THIRD QUARTER 2026 RESULTS

TACOMA, WASH. - Nov. 4, 2026 -- TrueBlue (NYSE:TBI) today announced its third quarter results for 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

•Revenue of $521 million, up 21 percent compared to the prior year period
•Net income of $9 million or $0.28 per share compared to net loss of $2 million or $0.06 per share in the prior year period
◦SG&A expense improved 3 percent to $89 million compared to $92 million in the prior year period
◦Adjusted EBITDA1 improved to $21 million compared to $11 million in the prior year period
◦Adjusted net income per share increased to $0.33 compared to $0.03 in the prior year period
•Cash of $20 million, debt of $102 million and $66 million of unused borrowing capacity, for total liquidity of $86 million at period end
◦Increased working capital by $35 million during the quarter.

Commentary

“We outperformed in the third quarter, exceeding expectations for both revenue and profitability as our strategic focus on end market expansion continues to produce meaningful results,” said Taryn Owen, President and CEO of TrueBlue. “We delivered our fifth consecutive quarter of top-line growth with increased operating leverage driven by strong execution in skilled verticals and continued growth in our core, on-demand business.”

Ms. Owen continued, “While recovery trends vary across verticals, geographies and services, we are seeing improvement in areas that are well aligned with our strategic positioning. Our results this quarter underscore the progress we are making against our priorities and the momentum building as we continue to expand in attractive markets, strengthen our sales model, and drive efficiency through technology and discipline to deliver long-term growth and margin expansion.”

Results

Third quarter revenue was $521 million, a 21 percent increase compared to the prior year period. Net income per diluted share was $0.28 compared to net loss per diluted share of $0.06 in the prior year period. Adjusted net income1 per diluted share was $0.33 compared to $0.03 in the prior year period.

About TrueBlue

TrueBlue (NYSE: TBI) is a leading provider of specialized workforce solutions. As The People Company®, we put people first–advancing our mission to connect people and work while delivering smart, scalable solutions that help businesses grow and communities thrive. Since our founding, TrueBlue has connected more than 10 million people with work and served over 3 million clients across a variety of industries. Powered by proprietary, digitally enabled platforms and decades of expertise, our brands–PeopleReady, PeopleScout, Staff Management | SMX, Centerline, SIMOS, and Healthcare Staffing Professionals–provide a full spectrum of flexible staffing, workforce management, and recruitment solutions that bring precision, speed and scale to the changing world of work. Learn more at www.trueblue.com.
