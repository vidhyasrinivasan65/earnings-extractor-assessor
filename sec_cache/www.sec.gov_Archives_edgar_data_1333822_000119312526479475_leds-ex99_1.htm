SemiLEDs Reports Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year End 2026

Financial Results

Hsinchu, Taiwan (November 27, 2026)— SemiLEDs Corporation (NASDAQ: LEDS), “SemiLEDs” or the “Company,” a developer and manufacturer of LED chips and LED components, today announced its financial results for the fourth quarter and full fiscal year 2026, ended August 31, 2026.

Revenue for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was $6.4 million, compared to $9.1 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026. GAAP net income attributable to SemiLEDs stockholders for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 was $640 thousand, or $0.08 per diluted share, compared to a net income of $1.5 million, or $0.18 per diluted share, in the third quarter of fiscal 2026.

GAAP gross margin for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 decreased to 23%, compared to 27% for the third quarter of fiscal 2026. Operating margin for the fourth quarter of fiscal 2026 decreased to 7%, compared to 16% for the third quarter of fiscal 2026. The Company’s cash and cash equivalents were $4.6 million at August 31, 2026, compared to $6.0 million at the end of the third quarter of fiscal 2026.

Revenues for fiscal year 2026 decreased to $19.1 million, compared to $43.0 million in fiscal year 2025. GAAP net income attributable to SemiLEDs stockholders for fiscal year 2026 was $817 thousand, or $0.10 per diluted share, compared to a net loss of $1.1 million, or $(0.15) per diluted share, in fiscal year 2025.

GAAP gross margin for fiscal year 2026 increased to 21%, compared to 6% for fiscal year 2025. Operating margin for fiscal year 2026 was 0.1%, compared to negative 4% in fiscal year 2025. The Company’s cash and cash equivalents were $4.6 million as of August 31, 2026, compared to $2.6 million as of August 31, 2025.

In fiscal years 2026 and 2025, the Company entered into a number of buy-sell orders for equipment that it purchased and then sold to its customers, and the Company anticipates continuing to receive buy-sell purchase orders in fiscal year 2027. As a result of these purchase orders and the associated uncertainty of the business, the Company’s revenue, cost of revenues, receivables, inventories and customer deposits may vary significantly over future quarters.

 

About SemiLEDs

SemiLEDs develops, manufactures, and sells light emitting diode (LED) chips, LED components, LED modules, sensor components incorporating LED or vertical-cavity surface-emitting laser (VCSEL), and systems. Its products are used for general lighting and specialty industrial applications, including ultraviolet, or UV, curing of polymers, LED light therapy in medical/cosmetic applications, counterfeit detection, germicidal and virucidal devices, LED lighting for horticulture applications, architectural lighting, entertainment lighting, and sensing of pressure or distance using LED or VCSEL.
