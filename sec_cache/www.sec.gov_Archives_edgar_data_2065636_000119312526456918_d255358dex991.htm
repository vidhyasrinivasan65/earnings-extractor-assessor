Results of Operations

Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 increased 65% year over year to $1,242 million, bringing year to date revenue to $2,870 million, or an increase of 69% year over year. This growth was primarily driven by both an increase in new construction activity and in existing infrastructure revenue.

Gross profit in the third quarter of 2026 was $221 million, bringing year to date results to $480 million. On an adjusted basis, or excluding non-cash compensation expense in cost of revenue, Adjusted Gross Profit was $215 million, bringing year to date 2026 results to $484 million.

Gross and Adjusted Gross Margin in the third quarter of 2026 were 17.8% and 17.3%, respectively, compared to 19.7% for both measures in the prior-year period. Through the first nine months of 2026, Gross and Adjusted Gross Margin were 16.7% and 16.9%, respectively, as compared to 18.9% for both measures in the prior year period.

Third quarter and nine months ending 2026 Gross and Adjusted Gross Margin variances year over year, primarily reflect the impact in 2025 from project performance and contingency release from later stage projects, the contribution of a significant repair job in 2025, and the classification change of both non-cash and annual incentive compensation expense from SG&A to Cost of Sales during 2026.

Net income was $123 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $70 million in the prior-year period. The increase primarily reflects higher operating income associated with revenue growth and lower interest expense following the repayment of term debt in connection with the February 2026 IPO. Net income year to date was $162 million, compared to income of $114 million in the prior-year period. Year to date net income reflects the benefit of higher operating income and lower interest expense, partially offset by an $11 million loss on extinguishment of the term debt repaid at the IPO and a $77 million one-time non-cash compensation charge related to the modification and accelerated vesting of legacy equity awards in connection with the IPO.

Adjusted EBITDA for the third quarter of 2026 was $173 million bringing year to date performance to $383 million, or 13.9% and 13.3% of revenue, respectively. See the reconciliation of net income to Adjusted EBITDA in the financial tables included in this press release.

Acquisition of ASB Energy Services

On October 30, 2026, SOLV completed the acquisition of ASB Energy Services, a Texas-based contractor specializing in substation foundations, civil construction and battery energy storage system infrastructure. The consideration for the acquisition included approximately $36 million in cash, net of cash acquired, and the issuance of 376,836 shares of Class A common stock. The addition of ASB Energy Services expands SOLV’s self-perform high-voltage construction capabilities and increases the Company’s presence in Texas and other high-growth regions. ASB serves customers across the data center, energy storage, renewable energy and industrial sectors and will operate as a SOLV Energy company.

Corporate Liquidity

SOLV ended the third quarter with approximately $609 million of liquidity, including $422 million of Cash and Cash Equivalents and $187 million of availability under the revolving credit facility after giving effect for approximately $13 million of outstanding letters of credit. Third quarter ending liquidity excludes the acquisition of ASB Energy Services as described above.

About SOLV

SOLV Energy (Nasdaq: MWH) is a leading provider of infrastructure services to the power industry, including engineering, procurement, construction, testing, commissioning, operations, maintenance and repowering. Since 2008, we have built over 24 GW of generating capacity. SOLV Energy also provides operations and maintenance (O&M) services to over 24 GW of generating capacity. In addition to EPC and O&M for utility-scale power plants and related T&D infrastructure, we offer large-scale repair, emergency response and repowering services and install end-to-end SCADA and network infrastructure solutions to maximize project performance and energy availability.
