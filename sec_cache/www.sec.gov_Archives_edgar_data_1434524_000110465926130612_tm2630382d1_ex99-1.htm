ClearSign Technologies Corporation Provides Third Quarter 2026 Update

 

Hosting Call at 5pm ET Today

 

TULSA, Okla., November 19, 2026 – ClearSign Technologies Corporation (Nasdaq: CLIR) (“ClearSign” or the “Company”), a leader in advanced combustion and sensing technologies that help industrial operators dramatically reduce emissions, increase efficiency and support the use of cleaner fuels including hydrogen, today provides an update on operations for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

“We have made considerable progress across all our major product lines this quarter,” said Jim Deller, Ph.D., Chief Executive Officer of ClearSign. “The biggest event of note is the commissioning and start-up of our 26 burner project on the Gulf Coast. We are excited to report that, while still in progress, indications from the client site are that the burners will be operating within specifications. We expect to complete the tuning of the burner and heater operation within the next week. We look forward to completing this start up and validating the formally reported emissions.

 

“Our M1 Series midstream burners are continuing to show market traction, with orders for five burners in the third quarter. We believe these orders underscore continued market demand for this product line and reflect growing adoption of the technology. Additionally, we have secured two more orders so far this quarter and believe we can continue to demonstrate consistent order flow.

 

“In terms of our flare product line, we have delivered a full system order and look forward to its startup, which is scheduled before year end. We believe this momentum, including these installations, positions the Company for sustained growth across its product portfolio.”

 

Strategic and Operational Highlights

 

Recent strategic and operational highlights including, and subsequent to, the third quarter of 2026:

 

Installation and Start Up of 26 Burner Project for Petrochemical Company on Texas Gulf Coast: The customer has completed the installation and the burners are operational. Initial internal testing indicates that emissions will be within guaranteed limits. Equipment tuning and the formal measurement of NOx emissions is expected to be completed in the coming week.

 

Secured Orders for Five M1 Series Burners in Q3 and Two Additional Orders to Date in Q4: ClearSign received orders from heater manufacturers for five M1 Series burners destined for gas producers in the Permian Basin region of West Texas. During the fourth quarter to date, the Company has secured two additional orders for M1 Series burners in the same region, demonstrating continued demand for its technology in this key market.

 

ClearSign and Kern Energy Selected for U.S. Department of Energy Industrial Technology Validation Program: The U.S. Department of Energy's ("DOE") Industrial Technologies Office ("ITO") selected ClearSign and Kern Energy for its Industrial Technology Validation ("ITV") program. The project is to support the installation and operation of ClearSign's Core™ Gen 2 Flex Fuel Process Burner at Kern Energy's facility and is expected to demonstrate ClearSign's technology designed to reduce air pollutants while allowing Kern Energy to use a refinery byproduct gas to offset natural gas consumption and reduce energy costs.

 

 

 

 

ClearSign Board of Directors Appoints Former ExxonMobil Global Technology Leader Larry Saddler: Mr. Saddler, who joined the Board in August 2026, is a retired ExxonMobil executive that has nearly 40 years of experience in heat transfer technology, fired equipment engineering, and global operations support. Throughout a distinguished career, he served in progressively senior technical leadership roles at ExxonMobil, including as Global Technology Sponsor for Heat Transfer, where he was responsible for, among other things, the oversight and management of the global fleet of fired equipment's technology, safety, environmental, reliability and margin performance.

About ClearSign Technologies Corporation

 

ClearSign Technologies Corporation designs and develops products and technologies for the purpose of decarbonization and improving key performance characteristics of industrial and commercial systems, including operational performance, energy efficiency, emission reduction, safety, the use of hydrogen as a fuel and overall cost-effectiveness. Our patented technologies, embedded in established OEM products as ClearSign Core™ and ClearSign Eye™ and other sensing configurations, enhance the performance of combustion systems and fuel safety systems in a broad range of markets, including the energy (upstream oil production and down-stream refining), commercial/industrial boiler, chemical, petrochemical, transport and power industries. For more information, please visit www.clearsign.com.
