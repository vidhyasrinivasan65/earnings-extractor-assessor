Magnera Reports Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year Results

Fourth Quarter Highlights
•
GAAP: Net sales of $851 million; Operating loss of $90 million (includes $106 million of goodwill and other impairment)
•
Non-GAAP: Adjusted EBITDA of $97 million
•
Record cash flow with cash from operations of $99 million
•
$57 million of debt repayments

Fiscal Year Highlights
•
GAAP: Net sales of $3.3 billion; Operating loss of $37 million (includes $106 million of goodwill and other impairment)
•
Non-GAAP: Adjusted EBITDA of $378 million
•
Free cash flow of $115 million represents a yield of over 25% as of year-end
•
$122 million of debt repayments

 
Curt Begle, Magnera’s CEO, commented: “We finished Fiscal 2026 with an impressive 8% Adjusted EBITDA improvement in the fourth quarter compared to last year and delivered strong overall results for the full year surpassing prior year by nearly 4%. Through the successful implementation of Project CORE, ongoing realization of synergies, and a relentless focus on operational excellence, we are optimizing our cost structure, enhancing profitability, and generating exceptional cash flow. Our teams delivered these results while navigating inflationary pressures and dynamic demand conditions across our end markets, demonstrating the resilience and flexibility of our operating model.

As we enter fiscal 2027, we remain focused on driving profitable growth and advancing our productivity and cost initiatives. While we remain mindful of the broader macroeconomic environment, we are confident that the strength of our team, our market positions, and our disciplined execution will continue to support sustainable earnings growth and strong cash generation.”

Financial Results – Fourth Quarter 2026

Consolidated Overview

The net sales increase included a $43 million increase in selling prices primarily due to the pass-through of higher raw material costs partially offset by a 4% organic volume decline. The volume decline was primarily attributed to softer healthcare demand in Americas and continued general market softness in Europe.

The Adjusted EBITDA was up 8% primarily as a result of favorable price cost spread of $9 million.

Americas

The net sales increase included a $19 million increase in selling prices primarily due to the pass-through of higher raw material costs partially offset by a 2% organic volume decline.

The Adjusted EBITDA improvement was primarily a result of favorable price cost spread of $4 million due to the realized benefits from project CORE and merger synergies.

Rest of World

The net sales were essentially flat as a $24 million increase in selling prices due to the pass-through of higher raw material costs was offset by a 6% organic volume decline.

The adjusted EBITDA improvement was primarily a result of favorable price cost spread of $5 million due to the realized benefits from project CORE and synergies partly offset by the negative impact from lower volumes.

About Magnera

Magnera Corporation (NYSE: MAGN) serves 1,000+ customers worldwide, offering a wide range of material solutions, including components for absorbent hygiene products, protective apparel, wipes, specialty building and construction products, and products serving the food and beverage industry. Operating across 44 global facilities, Magnera is supported by over 8,000+ employees. Magnera’s purpose is to better the world with new possibilities made real. For more than 160 years, the Company has delivered the material solutions their partners need to thrive. Through economic upheaval, global pandemics and changing end-user needs, we have consistently found ways to solve problems and exceed expectations. The distinct scale and comprehensive portfolio of products brings customers more materials and choices. Magnera builds personal partnerships that withstand an ever-changing world.

Visit Magnera.com for more information and follow @MagneraCorporation on social platforms.

Adjusted EBITDA is a measure widely used by investors, securities analysts, and other interested parties in our industry to measure a company’s performance.
