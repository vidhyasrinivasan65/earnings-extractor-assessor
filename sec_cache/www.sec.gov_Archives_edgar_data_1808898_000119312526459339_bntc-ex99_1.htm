Benitec Biopharma Releases First Quarter 2027 Fiscal-Year Financial Results and Provides Operational Update

•
Positive interim clinical results from the ongoing BB-301 Phase 1b/2a study for dysphagic patients with Oculopharyngeal Muscular Dystrophy (OPMD), presented as a late-breaking poster at the European Society of Gene and Cell Therapy (ESGCT) Annual Congress showed a 100% responder rate across all Cohort 1 (low dose) and Cohort 2 (high dose) patients
•
BB-301 pivotal study design to be finalized in the first calendar quarter of 2027, and the company remains on track to begin the pivotal trial in mid-2027

Well-capitalized with cash, as of September 30, 2026, of approximately $176 million

Benitec plans to transition its fiscal year-end for financial reporting from June 30 to December 31. In this release, operational and program guidance is presented on a calendar-year (CY) basis, and financial guidance is presented on a June 30 fiscal-year (FY) basis


 

HAYWARD, Calif., Nov.13, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Benitec Biopharma Inc. (NASDAQ: BNTC) (“Benitec” or the “Company”), a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing disease-modifying genetic medicines for life-threatening, genetically defined diseases, based on its proprietary “Silence and Replace” DNA-directed RNA interference (“ddRNAi”) platform, today announced financial results for its first fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided an update on recent clinical data presentations.

“This has been a productive period of continued clinical progress for BB-301,” said Jerel A. Banks, M.D., Ph.D., Executive Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Benitec. “We recently attended ESGCT and presented the interim clinical results for the three patients enrolled in Cohort 2 and the full clinical results for the six patients enrolled in Cohort 1 that have completed the 12-month study follow-up period. The consistency and durability of the clinical benefit observed to date continues to strengthen our conviction in the potential of BB-301 to meaningfully alter the course of OPMD-related dysphagia. We are grateful to the patients, families and investigators who have enabled this progress, and we look forward to confirming the pivotal study design in the first calendar quarter of 2027, with the study on track to commence in mid-2027.”

 

Clinical Highlights

The Company continues to advance BB-301 through clinical development in the ongoing Phase 1b/2a Clinical Study evaluating the safety and clinical efficacy of locally-administered BB-301 for the treatment of OPMD-related dysphagia.

Positive interim clinical results, presented as a late-breaking poster at the European Society of Gene and Cell Therapy (ESGCT) Annual Congress in Hamburg, Germany on October 28, 2026, showed a 100% responder rate across all Cohort 1 (low dose) and Cohort 2 (high dose) patients. There have been no Serious Adverse Events in any patient treated with BB-301 to date.

 

 

 

 

 

Financial Highlights

First Quarter 2027 FY Financial Results

Total expenses for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, were $14.5 million compared to $9.8 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. The Company incurred $8.6 million of research and development expenses compared to $3.4 million for the comparable quarter ended September 30, 2025. Research and development expenses relate primarily to ongoing clinical development of BB-301 for the treatment of OPMD. General and administrative expenses were $5.9 million compared to $6.4 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025.

Net loss for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, was $12.9 million compared to a loss of $9.0 million for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. Net loss per basic and diluted share was $(0.26) for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 compared to $(0.22) for the quarter ended September 30, 2025. As of September 30, 2026, the Company had $176.5 million in cash and cash equivalents.

Change in Fiscal Year to Calendar Year

The Company also announced today that it plans to change its existing fiscal year-end from June 30 to December 31 commencing with its first periodic report following the Form 10-Q for the fiscal quarter ended September 30, 2026. In this press release, operational and program guidance is presented on a calendar-year (CY) basis, and historical financial guidance is presented on a June 30 fiscal-year (FY) basis.

 

About BB-301

BB-301 is a novel, modified AAV9 capsid expressing a unique, single bifunctional construct promoting co-expression of both codon-optimized Poly-A Binding Protein Nuclear-1 (PABPN1) and two small inhibitory RNAs (siRNAs) against mutant PABPN1 (the causative gene for OPMD). The two siRNAs are modeled into microRNA backbones to silence expression of faulty mutant PABPN1, while allowing expression of the codon-optimized PABPN1 to replace the mutant with a functional version of the protein. BB-301 is administered locally, in a one-time procedure, to the muscles in the throat that drive the swallowing process, an approach designed to maximize local benefit and minimize systemic exposure. We believe the silence and replace mechanism of BB-301 is uniquely positioned for the treatment of OPMD by slowing or halting mutant PABPN1 expression while simultaneously providing a functional replacement protein. BB-301 has received Orphan Drug Designation from the EMA and Orphan Drug and Fast Track Designations from the FDA and is currently being evaluated in a Phase 1b/2a, first-in-human, open-label dose escalation study to evaluate the safety and clinical activity of intramuscular doses of BB-301 administered to subjects with OPMD (NCT06185673).

About Benitec Biopharma Inc.

Benitec Biopharma Inc. (NASDAQ: BNTC) is a clinical-stage biotechnology company developing disease-modifying genetic medicines designed to improve the lives of people with life-threatening, genetically defined diseases. The company’s proprietary “Silence and Replace” DNA-directed RNA interference (ddRNAi) platform combines RNA interference, or RNAi, with gene therapy to create medicines designed to facilitate sustained silencing of disease-causing genes and simultaneous delivery of replacement genes that restore normal cellular function following a single administration. The company’s lead investigational candidate, BB-301, is the first and only disease-modifying genetic medicine in clinical development for the treatment of Oculopharyngeal Muscular Dystrophy (OPMD)-related dysphagia. For additional information, visit www.benitec.com.
