HIVE DIGITAL TECHNOLOGIES LTD.

HIVE Reports Q2 Revenue of $84.9 Million with AI GPU Revenue
Expanding 70% as Dual-Engine Model Drives Resilience with $139
Million in Cash on the Balance Sheet

This news release constitutes a "designated news release" for the purposes of the Company's amended
and restated prospectus supplement dated June 16, 2026 to its short form base shelf prospectus dated
October 31, 2025.

San Antonio, TX, November 9, 2026 - HIVE Digital Technologies Ltd. (TSX: HIVE) (Nasdaq: HIVE) (BVC: HIVECO) (the "Company" or "HIVE"), a global provider of sustainable data center infrastructure, today announced its results for the second quarter of fiscal 2027, ended September 30, 2026. (All amounts are in US dollars unless otherwise indicated.)

HIVE generated revenue of $84.9 million, up 7.3% sequentially and down 2.7% year over year. BUZZ HPC revenue increased 70.7% year over year to $8.8 million, while gross operating margin² increased to $30.2 million, or 35.6% of revenue. Adjusted EBITDA² was $24.8 million, or 29.2% of revenue.  The Company reported a GAAP net loss of $78 million, including $54.9 million of depreciation and a $31.2 million non-cash impairment of mining equipment.

HIVE operates complementary digital infrastructure businesses: Tier I data centers that export Bitcoin mining compute and Tier III data centers that export AI computing infrastructure servicing enterprise customers. Powered by hydroelectricity, HIVE continues to expand its AI business to convert renewable energy into valuable computing capacity while managing the capital requirements and operating risks associated with both businesses.

"Our digital infrastructure platform combines established Bitcoin mining operations with a growing AI computing business," said Frank Holmes, Co-Founder and Executive Chairman. "Revenue was broadly stable year over year despite a 27% decline in Bitcoin prices, while HPC revenue increased more than 70%. Our approximately 300 MW Tier I platform in Paraguay and approximately 2% share of the global Bitcoin network's hashrate give us operating scale, while BUZZ is evaluating additional computing opportunities. We convert electrons into valuable intelligence and export computing power globally."

Q2 FY2027 Financial Highlights

• Total revenue: $84.9 million, up 7.3% sequentially and down 2.7% year over year. Both digital currency mining and HPC contributed to the sequential improvement.

• Digital currency revenue: $76.0 million, up 5.5% sequentially and down 7.4% year over year. Higher Bitcoin production supported the sequential improvement as average network difficulty declined to approximately 128 trillion from 135 trillion in Q1 FY2027. HIVE received 1,070 Bitcoin in rewards, compared with 1,004 in the preceding quarter.

• HPC revenue: $8.8 million for the quarter, up 25.3% sequentially and 70.7% year over year. Growth primarily reflected deployment of an NVIDIA B200 GPU cluster at HIVE's Manitoba, Canada, site, together with strong demand and pricing across GPU marketplaces. Pricing for BUZZ's bare-metal GPU platforms remained healthy, reflecting continued demand for AI computing capacity.

• Gross operating margin²: $30.2 million, or 35.6% of revenue, compared with $24.2 million, or 30.6%, in Q1 FY2027. See the calculation of gross operating margin included below in this press release.

• Selling, general and administrative expenses: $10.7 million, compared with $9.0 million in Q1 FY2027 and $7.8 million in Q2 FY2026. The sequential increase primarily reflected the Merritt facility lease associated with expansion of HIVE's HPC business.

• GAAP net loss: $78.7 million. Reported results included substantial non-cash charges: $54.9 million of depreciation, $31.2 million of equipment impairment, $8.6 million of share-based compensation and a $7.3 million change in the fair value of derivatives.  The non-cash equipment impairment followed a recoverability assessment under U.S. GAAP reflecting challenging Bitcoin mining economics and lower market values for ASIC equipment. The derivative fair-value loss primarily reflected the remeasurement of capped call transactions associated with convertible notes, partially offset by gains on other derivative instruments.

• Adjusted EBITDA²: $24.8 million, representing 29.2% of revenue and an 85% sequential increase.

• Liquidity: $139.0 million in cash and cash equivalents and $22.6 million in digital currencies as of September 30, 2026.

Dual-Engine Infrastructure and Economies of Scale

HIVE's Tier I Bitcoin infrastructure provides a scaled operating platform, while its Tier 3 AI infrastructure adds a growing source of contracted computing revenue. The Company's approximately 300 MW Paraguay Tier I platform forms part of its broader global infrastructure footprint. HIVE's Bitcoin operations represent approximately 2% of global network hashrate.

The Company currently operates approximately 440 MW of global power capacity and expects its energized footprint to reach approximately 540 MW following the planned energization of an additional 100 MW power purchase agreement at Yguazú in the fourth quarter of calendar 2026.

The additional 100 MW is currently unallocated, providing flexibility to direct power toward opportunities offering attractive returns across hashrate services, AI and HPC workloads, subject to the infrastructure and investment requirements of each use.

Contracted GPU Cloud ARR Reaches Approximately $185 Million

HIVE reports approximately $40 million of currently live GPU Cloud annualized run rate revenue ("ARR")¹ and approximately $185 million of contracted GPU Cloud ARR, inclusive of the live ARR.  Contracted ARR includes capacity that has not entered service and is not equivalent to current recognized revenue. The contracted amount includes the existing GPU Cloud platform and the following agreements:

• An approximately $225 million, three-year sovereign AI agreement with Bell AI Fabric supporting Cohere, covering 2,304 NVIDIA GB200 NVL72 GPUs.

• An approximately $350 million, five-year GPU cloud agreement with an investment-grade enterprise customer, covering 2,016 NVIDIA GB300 NVL72 GPUs.

These agreements provide visibility into potential future revenue as contracted capacity is deployed and enters service. HIVE is targeting approximately $200 million of GPU Cloud ARR by the fourth quarter of calendar 2026, subject to financing, market conditions, deployment schedules and customer acceptance.

Colocation Opportunity and Longer-Term Development

HIVE has signed a letter of intent for long-term colocation at its 32 MW Big Boden facility in Sweden, representing approximately $45 million of potential annual revenue if definitive agreements are executed and the project enters service.  This potential revenue is not included in contracted GPU Cloud ARR. The opportunity remains subject to definitive agreements, investment requirements and execution.

Beyond calendar 2026, HIVE's proposed Tier III development pipeline includes opportunities in New Brunswick, Toronto, Sweden together with the planned 320 MW Greater Toronto Area Gigafactory. Management has identified a longer-term target of approximately $700 million in total HPC ARR by year-end 2028.  This is a forward-looking target, not contracted revenue, and depends on financing, construction, customer commitments, commissioning, and market conditions.

Management Insights

Frank Holmes, Co-Founder and Executive Chairman, stated:

"Our presence in Canada, Sweden and Paraguay gives HIVE multiple avenues for growth. Canada offers a strong AI research and enterprise ecosystem, Sweden provides established digital infrastructure, and Paraguay brings abundant hydroelectric resources. We are combining those regional strengths with our GPU operating expertise and customer relationships.

In Canada, our relationship with Bell AI Fabric supporting Cohere positions BUZZ to serve growing demand for sovereign AI infrastructure. Governments and enterprises want advanced computing capacity while retaining control over their data and critical applications. In Sweden, our existing footprint provides a foundation for HPC expansion. In Paraguay, we are developing the technical capabilities and infrastructure for a longer-term AI opportunity alongside our established Bitcoin operations.

We believe the US election-fear-related rhetoric has contributed to negative sentiment toward data centers, and we expect sentiment could stabilize over the next six months. Our investment decisions remain grounded in customer demand, power economics, community and capital discipline. We continue to evaluate GPU pricing and demand as part of deployment decisions."

Aydin Kilic, President and CEO, stated:

"Our GPU Cloud business has reached approximately $40 million in live annual recurring revenue, with approximately $185 million in contracted ARR supported by three- and five-year agreements. With our recent $129 million financing to fund the GPU purchase for our three-year agreement with Cohere and Bell, our capital commitments for the 2,304 GB200 GPU cluster were satisfied as of September 30, 2026. We anticipate these GPUs will ship before calendar year-end, after which the lender's funds will be released from escrow to the GPU OEM as milestones are met.  We accomplished this while preserving a strong balance sheet, ending the quarter with $139 million in cash and $22.6 million in Bitcoin.

We recognize the macro headwinds in the AI infrastructure sector, and our primary objective is to protect our balance sheet and to maintain our cashflow and profitability. We note that GPU demand remains very high, and current GPU market prices (measured in dollars per GPU hour, which vary by GPU model) exceed contracted prices from six months ago. Our focus is on delivering these deployments, bringing contracted capacity into service and translating that growth into revenue and stronger earnings.

The supply of new GPU compute may be constrained, as new data center development throughout the U.S may face resistance following recent political shifts after the mid-term elections. In Canada, our sovereign AI thesis is being met with support. Notably, the Province of Ontario has released a data center playbook in August 2026, and the Premier's office recently committed to approving new data centers "sooner rather than later." HIVE has 400 MW of utility load across Canada earmarked for Tier III data centers for HPC applications, of which over 320 MW are in the Province of Ontario."

Darcy Daubaras, CFO, stated:

"Our second-quarter results reflected improved sequential operating performance, with revenue increasing 7% to $84.9 million, gross operating margin increasing to 35.6%, and Adjusted EBITDA reaching $24.8 million. Our GAAP net loss of $78.7 million included significant non-cash charges, notably $54.9 million of depreciation and a $31.2 million impairment of mining equipment following a U.S. GAAP recoverability assessment. We ended the quarter with $139.0 million in cash and $22.6 million in digital currencies. As we advance our contracted AI infrastructure deployments, our financial priorities remain disciplined capital allocation, liquidity management and translating new capacity into sustainable operating performance."

Craig Tavares, President & COO of BUZZ HPC, added:

"HIVE's sovereign AI data center footprint in Canada and Sweden could become increasingly valuable as political resistance and permitting delays constrain new computing capacity in the United States. This creates an opening for Canada to accelerate investment, attract customers, and narrow the AI infrastructure gap with the U.S. The Prime Minister's new fiscal policies could help turn that opportunity into growth by encouraging private capital and speeding infrastructure development. For BUZZ, our hydroelectric-powered sites, operating expertise, and growing GPU cloud platform position us to serve customers seeking reliable computing capacity and control over their data. Canada has an opportunity to skate to where the puck is going-and HIVE's BUZZ is positioned to help."

Non-GAAP Financial Measures


The Company uses gross operating margin and Adjusted EBITDA as supplemental non-GAAP financial measures to assess its operating performance. Management believes these measures provide useful information in evaluating the underlying performance of the Company's operations and facilitating comparisons between periods.

These measures are not recognized measures under U.S. GAAP, do not have standardized meanings prescribed by U.S. GAAP and may not be comparable to similarly titled measures presented by other companies. They should not be considered in isolation or as substitutes for financial measures prepared in accordance with U.S. GAAP.

Gross Operating Margin

The Company believes that, in addition to conventional measures prepared in accordance with US GAAP, it is helpful to management, the board and investors to use the gross operating margin to evaluate the Company's performance and its ability to generate cash flows and service debt.  The gross operating margin is defined as total revenue less direct cash costs, being operating and maintenance costs and high-performance computing service fees.

Adjusted EBITDA

The Company uses EBITDA and Adjusted EBITDA as a metric that is useful to management, the board and investors for assessing its operating performance on a cash basis before the impact of non-cash items and acquisition related activities.  EBITDA is net income or loss from operations, as reported in profit and loss, before finance income and expense, tax and depreciation and amortization. Adjusted EBITDA is EBITDA adjusted for by removing other non-cash items, including share-based compensation, finance expense, depreciation and one-time transactions.
