TriplePoint Venture Growth BDC Corp. Announces
Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
Declared Fourth Quarter 2026 Regular Distribution of $0.23 per Share
Seven Portfolio Companies Raised an Aggregate $2.6 Billion of Capital
Menlo Park, Calif., November 4, 2026 - TriplePoint Venture Growth BDC Corp. (NYSE: TPVG) (the “Company,” “TPVG,” “we,” “us,” or “our”), a leading financing provider to venture growth stage companies backed by a select group of venture capital firms in technology and other high growth industries, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026. The Company previously announced on October 14, 2026 that its Board of Directors declared its fourth quarter 2026 regular distribution of $0.23 per share.
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights
▪Signed $106.0 million of term sheets with venture growth stage companies at TriplePoint Capital LLC (“TPC”) and TPVG closed $43.0 million of new debt commitments;
▪Funded $0.7 million in debt investments to four portfolio companies, with an 11.2% weighted average annualized yield at origination. Subsequent to the end of the quarter, TPVG funded $57.4 million in new investments;
▪Ended the quarter with a debt investment portfolio at $665.3 million at cost;
▪Achieved a 12.4% weighted average annualized portfolio yield on debt investments for the quarter1;
▪Earned net investment income of $6.6 million, or $0.16 per share;
▪Net decrease in net assets resulting from operations of $1.7 million, or $0.04 per share;
▪Seven portfolio companies raised an aggregate $2.6 billion of capital in private financings during the quarter, five of which are active debt portfolio companies that raised an aggregate $1.5 billion;
▪Held debt investments in 50 portfolio companies, warrants in 120 portfolio companies and equity investments in 57 portfolio companies as of September 30, 2026;
▪Debt investment portfolio weighted average investment ranking of 2.24 as of quarter’s end;
▪Net asset value of $339.9 million, or $8.32 per share, as of September 30, 2026;
▪Ended the quarter with a gross leverage ratio of 1.18x and a net leverage ratio of 1.12x;
▪Our sponsor, TPC, purchased 313,865 shares of the Company’s common stock in a private transaction, bringing total shares held by TPC to 2,312,354, which represents 5.7% of the Company’s outstanding shares of common stock as of September 30, 2026;
▪Sold its investments in Prodigy Investments Limited to a third party for total cash consideration of $43.8 million, which reflected its fair value as of June 30, 2026 plus accrued cash interest;
▪Subsequent to quarter-end, our investment adviser, TriplePoint Advisers LLC (the “Adviser”) amended its existing income incentive fee waiver to waive, in full, its quarterly income incentive fee through the end of fiscal year 2027; and
▪Subsequent to quarter-end, declared a fourth quarter regular distribution of $0.23 per share, payable on December 30, 2026, bringing total declared distributions to $18.17 per share since the Company’s initial public offering.
Year to Date 2026 Highlights
▪Signed $668.9 million of term sheets with venture growth stage companies at TPC and TPVG closed $73.8 million of new debt commitments;
▪Funded $75.1 million in debt investments to 15 portfolio companies with a 12.8% weighted average annualized portfolio yield at origination, and funded $0.3 million in direct equity investments in private rounds of financing to five portfolio companies;
▪Earned net investment income of $24.0 million, or $0.59 per share;
▪Net increase in net assets resulting from operations of $15.1 million, or $0.37 per share;
▪Paid distributions of $0.75 per share;
▪12 debt portfolio companies raised an aggregate $2.8 billion of capital in private financings;
▪Achieved a 12.9% weighted average annualized portfolio yield on debt investments;
▪In April 2026, DBRS, Inc. confirmed TPVG’s investment grade rating, with a BBB (low) Long-Term Issuer rating, with a stable trend outlook;
▪The Company’s Board of Directors authorized a 12-month stock repurchase program for the purpose of repurchasing up to an aggregate of $12.5 million of its common stock in the open market; and
▪Estimated undistributed taxable earnings from net investment income (or “spillover income”) of $36.9 million, or $0.90 per share, as of September 30, 2026.

“We continue to advance our sector rotation and diversification strategy, as we invest in companies across AI and other attractive sectors and take advantage of strong demand for debt financing from high-quality, venture-growth stage companies,” said Jim Labe, chairman and chief executive officer of TPVG. “We remain focused on disciplined underwriting, further strengthening our portfolio and financial position, and capitalizing on market opportunities that enhance our earnings power and grow NAV over the long term.”
“We are pleased with the success of a number of TPVG portfolio companies raising capital,” said Sajal Srivastava, president and chief investment officer of the Company. “During the third quarter, seven of our portfolio companies raised $2.6 billion, of which five are active debt portfolio companies that raised an aggregate $1.5 billion.”
PORTFOLIO AND INVESTMENT ACTIVITY
During the three months ended September 30, 2026, the Company entered into $43.0 million of new debt commitments with four new and four existing portfolio companies, funded debt investments totaling $0.7 million to four portfolio companies and acquired warrant investments representing $0.6 million at fair value. Debt investments funded during the quarter carried a weighted average annualized portfolio yield of 11.2% at origination. During the quarter, the Company received $11.0 million of principal prepayments, $1.0 million of early repayments and $5.4 million of scheduled principal amortization. The weighted average annualized portfolio yield on debt investments for the third quarter was 12.4%1. The return on average equity for the third quarter was 7.4% based on net investment income. The Company calculates return on average equity as the annualized rate of net investment income recognized during the period divided by the Company’s average net asset value during the period.
As of September 30, 2026, the Company held debt investments in 50 portfolio companies, warrants in 120 portfolio companies and equity investments in 57 portfolio companies. The total cost and fair value of these investments were $749.3 million and $717.3 million, respectively.

As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s unfunded commitments totaled $171.6 million, of which $14.8 million was dependent upon portfolio companies reaching certain milestones. RESULTS OF OPERATIONS
Total investment and other income was $20.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, representing a weighted average annualized portfolio yield of 12.4% on debt investments, as compared to $22.7 million and 13.2% for the third quarter of 2025. The decrease in total investment and other income was primarily due to lower investment yields in part due to a lower Prime rate. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company’s total investment and other income was $65.1 million, as compared to $68.4 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025, representing a weighted average annualized portfolio yield on total debt investments of 12.9% and 14.0%, respectively.
For the third quarter of 2026, total operating expenses were $13.2 million as compared to $12.0 million, inclusive of an income incentive fee waiver of $2.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 consisted of $7.9 million of interest expense and amortization of fees, $3.6 million of base management fees, $0.7 million of Administration Agreement expenses and $1.1 million of general and administrative expenses. There were no income incentive fees for the three months ended September 30, 2026. Total operating expenses for the third quarter of 2025 consisted of $6.8 million of interest expense and amortization of fees, $3.4 million of base management fees, $0.6 million of Administration Agreement expenses and $1.2 million of general and administrative expenses. For the three months ended September 30, 2025, the Adviser earned and waived $2.1 million in income incentive fees. For each of the third quarters of 2026 and 2025, the Company recorded excise tax expenses of $0.4 million. The Adviser waived $3.2 million in income incentive fees earned for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, pursuant to a waiver agreement whereby the Adviser had agreed to waive, in full, any and all of the income incentive fee until and including the quarter ending December 31, 2026. The Company’s total operating expenses for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, inclusive of the $3.2 million income incentive fee waiver, were $40.1 million as compared to $34.9 million, inclusive of income incentive fee waivers of $3.3 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025, the Company recorded $1.0 million and $1.1 million, respectively, for an excise tax accrual.
For the third quarter of 2026, the Company recorded net investment income of $6.6 million, or $0.16 per share, as compared to $10.3 million, or $0.26 per share, for the third quarter of 2025. The decrease in net investment income between periods was driven primarily by lower total investment and other income and higher interest expense. Net investment income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was $24.0 million, or $0.59 per share, compared to $32.3 million, or $0.80 per share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company recognized net realized losses on investments of $26.4 million, resulting primarily from the sale of the Company’s debt, warrant and equity investments in one portfolio company and the write-off of a debt investment in one portfolio company, partially offset by net realized gains from the realization of a warrant investment in one portfolio company and equity investments in two portfolio companies. During the third quarter of 2025, the Company recognized net realized losses on investments of $0.7 million from the dissolution of one portfolio company.
Net change in unrealized gains on investments for the third quarter of 2026 was $18.1 million, consisting of $26.1 million of net unrealized gains from the reversal of previously recorded unrealized losses on investments realized during the period and $5.4 million of net unrealized gains on the existing warrant and equity portfolio resulting from fair value adjustments, partially offset by $13.0 million of net unrealized losses on the existing debt investment portfolio resulting from fair value adjustments and $0.4 million of net unrealized losses from foreign currency adjustments. Net change in unrealized gains on investments for the third quarter of 2025 was $5.6 million. The Company’s net realized and unrealized losses were $8.9 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to net realized and unrealized gains of $8.8 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
The Company’s net decrease in net assets resulting from operations for the third quarter of 2026 was $1.7 million, or $0.04 per share, as compared to a net increase in net assets resulting from operations of $15.2 million, or $0.38 per share, for the third quarter of 2025. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, the Company’s net increase in net assets resulting from operations was $15.1 million, or $0.37 per share, as compared to a net increase in net assets resulting from operations of $41.1 million, or $1.02 per share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.
CREDIT QUALITY
The Adviser maintains a credit watch list with portfolio companies placed into one of five credit risk categories, with Clear, or 1, being the best rating and Red, or 5, being the lowest. Generally, all new loans receive an initial grade of White, or 2, unless the portfolio company’s credit quality meets the characteristics of another credit category.
As of September 30, 2026, the weighted average investment ranking of the Company’s debt investment portfolio was 2.24, as compared to 2.28 at the end of the prior quarter. During the quarter ended September 30, 2026, portfolio company credit category changes, excluding fundings and repayments, consisted of the following: one portfolio company with a principal balance of $28.0 million was upgraded from Yellow (3) to White (2), and two portfolio companies with an aggregate principal balance of $33.8 million were downgraded from White (2) to Yellow (3).

ABOUT TRIPLEPOINT VENTURE GROWTH BDC CORP.
TriplePoint Venture Growth BDC Corp. is an externally-managed business development company focused on providing customized debt financing with warrants and direct equity investments primarily to venture growth stage companies in technology and other high growth industries backed by a select group of venture capital firms. The Company’s sponsor, TriplePoint Capital, is a Sand Hill Road-based global investment platform which provides customized debt financing, leasing, direct equity investments and other complementary solutions to venture capital-backed companies in technology and other high growth industries at every stage of their development with unparalleled levels of creativity, flexibility and service. For more information about TriplePoint Venture Growth BDC Corp., visit https://www.tpvg.com. For more information about TriplePoint Capital, visit https://www.triplepointcapital.com.
