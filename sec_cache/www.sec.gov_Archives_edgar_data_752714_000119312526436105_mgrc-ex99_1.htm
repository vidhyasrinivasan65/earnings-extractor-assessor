FOR RELEASE October 28, 2026

McGrath Announces Results for Third Quarter 2026

 

 

Livermore, CA - October 28, 2026 – McGrath RentCorp (“McGrath” or the “Company”) (Nasdaq: MGRC), a leading business-to-business rental company in North America, today announced total revenues for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 of $274.6 million, an increase of 7% compared to the third quarter of 2025. The Company reported net income of $48.8 million, or $2.00 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2026, compared to net income of $42.3 million, or $1.72 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. The increases in net income and earnings per diluted share for the quarter were primarily attributed to higher gross profit on rental operations and sales revenues when compared to the third quarter of 2025.

 

THIRD QUARTER 2026 YEAR-OVER-YEAR Company HIGHLIGHTS:

•
Rental operations revenues increased 6% to $189.5 million.
•
Sales revenues increased 9% to $82.9 million.
•
Total revenues increased 7% to $274.6 million.
•
Income from operations increased 11% to $73.9 million.
•
Adjusted EBITDA1 increased 10% to $106.5 million.
•
Dividend rate of $0.495 per share for the third quarter 2026. On an annualized basis, this dividend represents a 1.7% yield on the October 27, 2026 close price of $119.56 per share.

Phil Hawkins, President and CEO of McGrath, made the following comments:

“We were pleased with our third quarter results. The 7% increase in companywide revenues was primarily driven by higher rental operations, reflecting steady progress at Mobile Modular and TRS-RenTelco.

 

Modular rental revenues increased 4% compared to last year, with continued growth from our commercial customer base. Consistent with the first half of this year, we experienced positive business momentum during the quarter, particularly with large commercial projects and progress with our regional expansion efforts. We responded to this momentum with additional capital investments in new rental fleet.

Portable Storage rental revenues decreased 3% as market demand conditions remained soft. Consistent with the first half of this year, higher costs for equipment preparation, trucking and sales coverage continued to pressure margins in the quarter.

 

TRS had another impressive quarter with rental revenues increasing 26%. Demand remained robust throughout the quarter, and the business continued to benefit from projects supporting buildout of new data centers. We made additional investments in rental fleet to support the strong market conditions and positive outlook for the business.

 

Our updated outlook for the year reflects continued positive rental operations momentum and investment at Mobile Modular and TRS, partly offset by lower Modular new equipment sales. We are focused on disciplined operational execution and capitalizing on our rental operations momentum through the remainder of the year.”

 

 

 

Division HIGHLIGHTS:

All comparisons presented below are for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 to the quarter ended September 30, 2025 unless otherwise indicated.

Mobile Modular

For the third quarter of 2026, the Company’s Mobile Modular division reported Adjusted EBITDA of $62.6 million, a decrease of $1.9 million, or 3%, when compared to the same quarter in 2025.

•
Rental revenues increased 4% to $86.9 million, depreciation expense increased 11% to $12.0 million and other direct costs increased 7% to $25.9 million, which resulted in an increase in gross profit on rental revenues of 2% to $49.0 million.
•
Rental related services revenues was comparable to the prior year at $44.6 million, primarily attributable to higher delivery and dismantle revenues, partly offset by lower site related service revenues, with associated gross profit increasing 7% to $15.7 million.
•
Sales revenues decreased 10% to $47.1 million, primarily due to lower new equipment sales. Lower sales revenues and a comparable gross margin on sales of 36% in 2026, resulted in a 10% decrease in gross profit on sales revenues to $16.8 million.
•
Selling and administrative expenses increased 8% to $38.3 million, when compared to the prior year.

Portable storage

For the third quarter of 2026, the Company’s Portable Storage division reported Adjusted EBITDA of $7.4 million, a decrease of $1.9 million, or 20%, when compared to the same quarter in 2025.

•
Rental revenues decreased 3% $16.8 million, depreciation expense increased 5% to $1.1 million, and other direct costs increased 1% to $2.2 million, which resulted in a decrease in gross profit on rental revenues of 4% to $13.4 million.
•
Rental related services revenues increased 5% to $4.4 million, primarily attributable to higher delivery and return delivery activities. Gross margin on rental related services was negative 25% compared to negative 13% in 2025, primarily due to higher trucking related costs, resulting in a $0.6 million higher gross loss of $1.1 million.
•
Sales revenues decreased 16% to $2.3 million. Gross margin on sales decreased 3% to 37%, compared to 40% in 2025, resulting in a $0.2 million decrease in gross profit on sales revenues to $0.8 million.
•
Selling and administrative expenses increased 5% to $8.3 million, when compared to the prior year.

TRS-RenTelco

For the third quarter of 2026, the Company’s TRS-RenTelco division reported Adjusted EBITDA of $25.7 million, an increase of 27% when compared to the same quarter in 2025.

•
Rental revenues increased 26% to $35.4 million, depreciation expense increased 15% and other direct costs increased 14%, resulting in a 42% increase in gross profit on rental revenues to $17.2 million.
•
Sales revenues increased 7% to $7.9 million. Gross margin on sales in 2026 was 56%, unchanged from 2025, resulting in an increase in gross profit on sales revenues of 8% to $4.5 million.
•
Selling and administrative expenses increased 15% to $8.2 million, when compared to the prior year.

ABOUT MCGRATH:

McGrath RentCorp (Nasdaq: MGRC) is a leading business-to-business rental company in North America with a strong record of profitable business growth. Founded in 1979, McGrath’s operations are centered on modular solutions through its Mobile Modular and Mobile Modular Portable Storage businesses. In addition, its TRS-RenTelco business offers electronic test equipment rental solutions. The Company’s rental product offerings and services are part of the circular supply economy, helping customers work more efficiently, and sustainably manage their environmental footprint. With over 40 years of experience, McGrath’s success is driven by a focus on exceptional customer experiences. This focus has underpinned the Company’s long-term financial success and supported over 35 consecutive years of annual dividend increases to shareholders, a rare distinction among publicly listed companies.

McGrath is headquartered in Livermore, California. Additional information about McGrath and its businesses is available at mgrc.com and investors.mgrc.com.

Reconciliation of Adjusted EBITDA to the most directly comparable GAAP measures

To supplement the Company’s financial data presented on a basis consistent with accounting principles generally accepted in the United States of America (“GAAP”), the Company presents “Adjusted EBITDA”, which is defined by the Company as net income before interest expense, provision for income taxes, depreciation, amortization, non-cash impairment costs, share-based compensation, transaction costs, gains on property sales and non-operating transactions. The Company presents Adjusted EBITDA as a financial measure as management believes it provides useful information to investors regarding the Company’s liquidity and financial condition and because management, as well as the Company’s lenders, use this measure in evaluating the performance of the Company.

Management uses Adjusted EBITDA as a supplement to GAAP measures to further evaluate period-to-period operating performance, compliance with financial covenants in the Company’s revolving lines of credit and senior notes and the Company’s ability to meet future capital expenditure and working capital requirements. Management believes the exclusion of non-cash charges and non-recurring transactions, including share-based compensation, transaction costs and gains on property sales is useful in measuring the Company’s cash available for operations and performance of the Company. Because management finds Adjusted EBITDA useful, the Company believes its investors will also find Adjusted EBITDA useful in evaluating the Company’s performance.

Adjusted EBITDA should not be considered in isolation or as a substitute for net income, cash flows, or other consolidated income or cash flow data prepared in accordance with GAAP or as a measure of the Company’s profitability or liquidity. Adjusted EBITDA is not in accordance with or an alternative for GAAP and may be different from non−GAAP measures used by other companies. Unlike EBITDA, which may be used by other companies or investors, Adjusted EBITDA does not include share-based compensation charges, transaction costs, gains on property sales and non-operating transactions. The Company believes that Adjusted EBITDA is of limited use in that it does not reflect all of the amounts associated with the Company’s results of operations as determined in accordance with GAAP and does not accurately reflect real cash flow. In addition, other companies may not use Adjusted EBITDA or may use other non-GAAP measures, limiting the usefulness of Adjusted EBITDA for purposes of comparison. The Company’s presentation of Adjusted EBITDA should not be construed as an inference that the Company will not incur expenses that are the same as or similar to the adjustments in this presentation. Therefore, Adjusted EBITDA should only be used to evaluate the Company’s results of operations in conjunction with the corresponding GAAP measures. The Company compensates for the limitations of Adjusted EBITDA by relying upon GAAP results to gain a complete picture of the Company’s performance. Because Adjusted EBITDA is a non-GAAP financial measure, as defined by the SEC, the Company includes in the tables below reconciliations of Adjusted EBITDA to the most directly comparable financial measures calculated and presented in accordance with GAAP.
