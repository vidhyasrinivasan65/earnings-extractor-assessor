bioAffinity Technologies Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Continued Momentum in CyPath® Lung Commercialization

 

CyPath® Lung test unit sales double year to date over 2025, with solid gains in physician utilization and recurring orders; testing revenue increases 79% year-to-date

 

Company advances precision medicine pipeline with new asthma and COPD research, collaboration with Pictor and continued development of topical skin cancer therapeutics

 

SAN ANTONIO, Texas – November 6, 2026 – bioAffinity Technologies, Inc. (Nasdaq: BIAF; BIAFW), a biotechnology company developing noninvasive healthcare solutions for the early detection and monitoring of lung disease and topically delivered therapeutics for skin cancer, today reported financial results and business highlights for the third quarter (Q3) ended September 30, 2026.

 

Q3 2026 Highlights and Recent Events

 

●CyPath® Lung test unit sales increased 100% through Q3 compared to the first nine months of 2025.
●CyPath Lung testing revenue for the first nine months of 2026 increased 79% to approximately $1.1 million, compared to the same period in 2025.
●In July 2026, the Company surpassed the total number of CyPath Lung test reports delivered to physicians during 2025.
●The number of ordering physicians increased by 89% in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025, continuing to expand clinical utilization of CyPath Lung. Physicians already ordering CyPath Lung tests increased their volume by 48% year over year.
●The Company expanded its government healthcare distribution network and commercial reach through an agreement with AvMEDICAL, a Service-Disabled Veteran-Owned Small Business and established distributor of medical-surgical and laboratory products and services to government agencies, including the Department of Veterans Affairs (VA).
●The ongoing longitudinal clinical trial designed to evaluate the clinical performance of CyPath Lung continued to advance. As of September 30, 2026, 11 clinical sites were active, including nine VA and military medical centers that had begun patient enrollment. The John P. Murtha Cancer Center Research Program at the Uniformed Services University of the Health Sciences is providing support and funding for the trial.

 

-1-
 

 

●In July, the Company published a comprehensive clinical review and white paper authored by Chief Medical Officer Gordon H. Downie, MD, PhD, presenting a practical framework for incorporating CyPath Lung into pulmonology and oncology practices. Dr. Downie presented the framework to physicians in October at the annual meeting of the American College of Chest Physicians (CHEST 2026).
●The Company advanced CyPath Lung for potential use in surveillance of lung cancer survivors as a complement to imaging to monitor patients for cancer recurrence and new primary lung cancers.
●The Company presented positive research results at CHEST 2026 relating to its development of noninvasive tests to characterize lung inflammation and investigate biologic drug targets for asthma and COPD therapies. Further development and validation are underway to determine whether this approach can identify patients who may benefit from specific biologic therapies or support monitoring of lung inflammation over time.
●The Company announced a collaboration with Pictor®, Inc., a targeted proteomic platform company, to support development and potential commercialization of bioAffinity Technologies’ next-generation diagnostic tests designed to provide a more complete picture of lung inflammation in patients with asthma and COPD.
●The Company continued research into potential topically delivered therapeutics for squamous and basal cell skin cancers. Preliminary research findings have demonstrated that self-delivering, stabilized small interfering RNAs (siRNAs) selectively kill squamous and basal carcinoma cells in vitro while sparing noncancerous cutaneous cells.
●In September, bioAffinity announced the grant of a Hong Kong patent covering CyPath Lung’s proprietary flow cytometry + AI platform for predicting the likelihood of lung cancer by characterizing cell populations in sputum. In October, the Company received a Notice of Allowance from the Japan Patent Office indicating its intention to grant a patent for the method of predicting the likelihood of lung cancer used by the CyPath Lung test.
●The Company raised an aggregate of $8.0 million in gross proceeds through a private placement offering in August 2026 and a registered direct offering and concurrent private placement in October 2026 for working capital and general corporate purposes, including support for the ongoing commercialization of CyPath Lung.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Total revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, was approximately $1.1 million, compared with approximately $1.4 million for the same period in 2025. The decrease is due primarily to a decrease in average reimbursement per CyPath Lung test and a slight decline in total cases completed at Precision Pathology Laboratory Services (PPLS) compared to the same period in 2025, in part due to internal business changes at three PPLS research clients. Multiple accounts previously serviced under these clients have since returned to PPLS. The Company is working to restore additional accounts and improve reimbursement levels.

 

Net loss for the quarter ended September 30, 2026, was approximately $5.3 million, compared with approximately $5.1 million for the third quarter of 2025. The increase in net loss is primarily due to the increase in loss from operations of approximately $3.5 million from approximately $2.3 million in the prior-year quarter, partially offset by a $1.2 million loss from the change in fair value of warrants, compared with a $2.7 million loss in the prior-year quarter.

 

Cash and cash equivalents were approximately $2.6 million as of September 30, 2026, compared with approximately $6.4 million at December 31, 2025. Subsequent to quarter-end, the Company completed a financing that generated approximately $4.0 million in gross proceeds.

 

Operating expenses for the third quarter of 2026 were approximately $4.6 million, compared with approximately $3.7 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

●Direct costs and expenses were approximately $1.1 million, compared with $942,000 in the prior-year period. The increase primarily reflected additional personnel to process CyPath Lung commercial tests and tests associated with the longitudinal clinical study.
●Research and development expenses increased 18% year over year to approximately $389,000, primarily due to laboratory supplies and costs associated with relocating laboratory facilities in June 2026.

●Clinical development expenses increased 75% to approximately $251,000, compared with approximately $143,000 in the third quarter of 2025. The increase primarily reflected the initiation of clinical trial sites and patient enrollment in the longitudinal clinical study.
●Selling, general and administrative expenses increased 28% to approximately $2.8 million, compared with approximately $2.2 million in the prior-year period. The increase primarily reflected additional sales and marketing personnel and support services to expand commercialization of CyPath Lung.

 

About CyPath Lung

 

CyPath Lung by bioAffinity Technologies is a noninvasive test designed to improve the early detection of lung cancer in patients at high risk for the disease. CyPath Lung analyzes the lung microenvironment by using advanced flow cytometry and proprietary artificial intelligence (AI) to identify cell populations in patient sputum that indicate malignancy. The test incorporates a novel fluorescent porphyrin that is preferentially taken up by cancer and cancer-related cells. In a published clinical trial of high-risk patients, CyPath Lung demonstrated 92% sensitivity, 87% specificity, 88% accuracy and 99% negative predictive value (NPV) in detecting lung cancer in patients at high risk for the disease who had small indeterminate lung nodules less than 20 millimeters. The high NPV gives physicians greater confidence that a negative result is truly negative, potentially sparing patients from unnecessary invasive and costly procedures. CyPath Lung is marketed as a Laboratory Developed Test (LDT) and is not intended for use as a sole diagnostic tool and should be considered alongside other clinical findings. For more information, visit www.cypathlung.com.

 

About bioAffinity Technologies, Inc.

 

bioAffinity Technologies, Inc. addresses the need for noninvasive diagnosis of early-stage cancer and other diseases of the lung and broad-spectrum cancer treatments. The Company’s first product, CyPath Lung, is a noninvasive test that has shown high sensitivity, specificity and accuracy for the detection of early-stage lung cancer. CyPath Lung is marketed as a Laboratory Developed Test (LDT) by Precision Pathology Laboratory Services, a subsidiary of bioAffinity Technologies. LDTs are overseen under the Clinical Laboratory Improvement Amendments (CLIA), which are administered by the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services. For more information, visit www.bioaffinitytech.com.
