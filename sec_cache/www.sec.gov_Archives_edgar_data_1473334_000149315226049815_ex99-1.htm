XMax Inc. Announces Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

LOS ANGELES, November 3, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) — XMax Inc. (NASDAQ: XMAX, “XMAX” or the “Company”), a distributor of contemporary furniture and artificial intelligence software and platform-based service provider, today announced selected preliminary unaudited financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026.

 

Preliminary Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights:

 

●Net Sales: Approximately $6.42 million, compared to $9.76 million in the third quarter of 2025.

 

●Gross Profit: Approximately $0.89 million, representing a gross margin of approximately 13.9%, compared to 10.1% in the third quarter of 2025.

 

●Net Loss: Expected to be in the range of $12.10 million to $13.10 million, primarily reflecting a non-cash unrealized loss

 

Preliminary Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 Financial Highlights:

 

●Net Sales: Approximately $10.94 million, compared to $14.95 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

 

●Gross Profit: Approximately $2.87 million, representing a gross margin of approximately 26.2%, compared to 23.2% for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.

 

●Net Income: Expected to be in the range of $19.50 million to $20.50 million, primarily reflecting a non-cash unrealized gain, partially offset by income tax expense.

 

Management Commentary

 

“The third quarter was a period of transition for XMax as we continued to build our AI business alongside our furniture distribution business,” said Andy Lu, Chief Executive Officer of XMax. “We have continued to make commercial progress in AI,” Mr. Lu added. “Since mid-September, we have announced our cooperation with apM Group in Korea, our entry into an agreement to acquire Hexa Creation Inc., and an AI infrastructure services agreement that we expect to generate over US$100 million in revenue over the next five years, subject to satisfaction of certain closing conditions. Our focus now is on executing on and commercializing these initiatives. Our Board of Directors recently approved a share repurchase program of up to $30 million, which reflects our confidence in our long-term strategy and our commitment to disciplined capital allocation.”

The Company expects to report its complete financial results for the third quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2026 in its Quarterly Report on Form 10-Q, which it expects to file with the Securities and Exchange Commission on or before November 16, 2026.

 

About XMax Inc.

 

Headquartered in Commerce, California, XMax Inc. (NASDAQ: XMAX), formerly known as Nova LifeStyle, Inc., is a diversified company engaged in the design, sourcing, and distribution of contemporary furniture, as well as the development of artificial intelligence technologies and applications. The Company operates through an established global network of suppliers, distributors, and e-commerce channels, serving a broad customer base. In addition, the Company is expanding into artificial intelligence technologies, including AI software and platform-based services through its wholly owned subsidiaries XMax AI Inc. and Elonx AI Holdings PTE. LTD., to support future growth. By leveraging emerging technologies, the Company aims to drive diversification and long-term value creation.
