Star Equity Holdings Reports 2026 Third Quarter Results

Recently Closed Two Accretive Bolt-On Acquisitions
Launched Process to Monetize Real Estate Assets

OLD GREENWICH, CT - November 11, 2026 - Star Equity Holdings, Inc. (Nasdaq: STRR and STRRP) ("Star" or the "Company"), a diversified holding company, announced today financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

2026 Third Quarter Summary

•Revenue of $53.9 million increased 12.4% from the third quarter of 2025.
•Gross profit of $23.2 million increased 12.6% from the third quarter of 2025.
•Net loss attributable to common shareholders was $2.2 million, or $0.60 per diluted share, compared to net loss attributable to common shareholders of $1.8 million, or $0.56 per diluted share, for the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net loss per diluted share (non-GAAP measure)* was $0.26 compared to adjusted net income per diluted share of $0.08 in the third quarter of 2025. Pro forma adjusted net income per diluted share was $0.18 in the third quarter of 2025.
•Adjusted EBITDA (non-GAAP measure)* increased to $1.5 million versus adjusted EBITDA of $1.3 million in the third quarter of 2025; pro forma adjusted EBITDA was $3.1 million in the third quarter of 2025.
•Total cash including restricted cash was $7.8 million at September 30, 2026.

Jeff Eberwein, CEO of Star, noted, “In the third quarter, Energy Services gross profit and adjusted EBITDA increased on a pro forma basis, and in October we expanded the division by acquiring a machine shop in Vernal, Utah adding in-house machining, repair, and rebuild capabilities to our existing operation there. In Building Solutions, results were below the prior year quarter on a pro forma basis, reflecting continued softness in the residential and multifamily new construction markets. In addition, a large KBS Builders project, for which we received a non-refundable deposit, has shifted from 2026 to 2027. Despite these headwinds, the division’s backlog, which excludes this project, grew to $11.4 million from $10.6 million at the end of the second quarter and new orders were above $17.0 million for the second quarter in a row.”

Jake Zabkowicz, Global CEO of Hudson Talent Solutions (“HTS”), added, “Despite continued macroeconomic uncertainty and pressure in the professional talent market, HTS limited its year-over-year gross profit decline to 2%, supported by 4% gross profit growth in the Americas. In the third quarter, we invested $1.4 million in digital offerings, expansion into new geographies, and other growth initiatives, while increasing our use of agentic AI and automation to enhance recruiter productivity and candidate matching. In November, we acquired Cornerstone RPO, which bolsters our presence in the U.S., the world’s largest RPO market, and adds established enterprise client relationships and experienced U.S. sales leadership. Additionally, we moved up to No. 3 globally, a significant improvement versus history,
in HRO Today’s 2026 Global RPO Baker’s Dozen Customer Satisfaction Ratings. We believe this recognition and our investments in growth initiatives position us well to win new business going forward.”

Mr. Eberwein concluded, “In the third quarter, we generated $0.9 million of cash flow from operations and repurchased approximately $0.3 million of our common stock. We remain focused on disciplined execution, rigorous cost management, and returns‑driven capital allocation. We continue to evaluate M&A opportunities across all three operating divisions while also working to make progress on monetizing our non-cash-generating assets including two real estate holdings where we expect to receive gross proceeds of over $9.0 million from two sale-leaseback transactions. We believe we are well positioned to improve profitability in 2027 and create significant value for our stockholders over the long term.”

Division Highlights

Building Solutions

Third quarter Building Solutions revenue was $16.3 million and gross profit was $3.3 million. Adjusted EBITDA was $0.7 million.

Pro forma ("PF")(1) Building Solutions revenue was $21.4 million for the third quarter of 2025, and PF gross profit was $5.3 million. PF adjusted EBITDA was $2.6 million.

Building Solutions quarter-end backlog was $11.4 million, up from $10.6 million at Q2 2026, and the trailing 12-month book-to-bill ratio was 0.86.

Business Services

Third quarter 2026 Business Services revenue was $33.8 million, compared to $37.0 million in the prior year quarter, while gross profit was $18.2 million, compared to $18.6 million a year ago. Business Services adjusted EBITDA was $1.0 million, compared to adjusted EBITDA of $1.7 million in the prior year quarter. In Q3 2026, the Business Services division invested $1.4 million towards growth-related investments in its digital offerings, new geographies, and other items compared to $1.0 million in Q3 2025.

Regionally, Americas gross profit grew 4%, while EMEA and Asia Pacific gross profit declined by 9% and 5%, respectively.

Energy Services

Third quarter 2026 Energy Services revenue was $3.7 million. Gross profit was $1.8 million. Energy Services adjusted EBITDA was $1.1 million in the third quarter.

PF Energy Services revenue for the third quarter of 2025 was $3.7 million and PF gross profit was $1.5 million. Third quarter 2025 PF adjusted EBITDA was $1.0 million.


(1) Pro forma Building Solutions and Energy Services results for the full third quarter of 2025.

Corporate Costs

In the third quarter of 2026, the Company's corporate costs were $1.8 million, up from $1.2 million in the prior year quarter, but down $0.8 million on a PF basis. The decrease on a PF basis was primarily driven by synergies realized from the Merger. Corporate costs in the third quarter of 2026 and 2025 excluded non-recurring expenses of $0.7 million and $1.3 million, respectively.

Liquidity and Capital Resources

The Company ended the third quarter of 2026 with $7.8 million in cash, including $1.6 million in restricted cash. The Company generated $0.9 million in cash flow from operations during the third quarter of 2026 compared to using $2.7 million in cash flow from operations in the third quarter of 2025.

Share Repurchase Program

In the third quarter of 2026, the Company repurchased 28,736 shares for approximately $0.3 million. As of the end of the third quarter of 2026, the Company has approximately $1.3 million remaining under its $3 million repurchase program authorized in September 2025 and continues to view share repurchases as an attractive use of capital.

NOL Carryforward

As of December 31, 2025, Star had $215 million of usable net operating losses (“NOL”) in the U.S., which the Company considers to be a very valuable asset for its stockholders. In order to protect the value of the NOL for all stockholders, the Company has a rights agreement and charter amendment in place that limit beneficial ownership of Star common stock to 4.99%. Stockholders who wish to own more than 4.99% of Star common stock, or who already own more than 4.99% of Star common stock and wish to buy more, may only acquire additional shares with the Board’s prior written approval.

About Star Equity Holdings, Inc.
Star Equity Holdings, Inc. is a diversified holding company that seeks to build long-term shareholder value by acquiring, managing, and growing businesses with strong fundamentals and market opportunities. Its current structure comprises four divisions: Building Solutions, Business Services, Energy Services, and Investments. For more information visit www.starequity.com.

Building Solutions
The Building Solutions division operates in three specialties: (i) modular building manufacturing; (ii) structural wall panel and wood foundation manufacturing, including building supply distribution operations; and (iii) glue-laminated timber (glulam) column, beam, and truss manufacturing.

Business Services
The Business Services division provides flexible and scalable recruitment solutions to a global clientele, servicing organizations at all levels, from entry-level positions to the C-suite. The division focuses on mid-market and enterprise organizations worldwide, partnering consultatively with talent acquisition, HR, and procurement leaders to build diverse, high-impact teams and drive business success.

Energy Services
The Energy Services division engages in the rental, sale, and repair of downhole tools used in the oil and gas, geothermal, mining, and water-well industries.

Investments
The Investments division manages and finances the Company’s real estate assets as well as its investment positions in private and public companies.
