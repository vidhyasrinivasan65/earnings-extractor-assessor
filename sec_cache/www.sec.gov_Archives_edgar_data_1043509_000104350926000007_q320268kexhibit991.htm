Sonic Automotive Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

All-Time Record Quarterly Total Revenues Of $4.0 Billion, All-Time Record Quarterly Gross Profit Of $617.5 Million

EchoPark Segment Retail Unit Sales Volume Increased 22% Year-Over-Year, Driving Third Quarter Record Segment Gross Profit

Powersports Segment Achieved Record Unit Sales Volume At The 86th Annual Sturgis Motorcycle Rally, Driving All-Time Record Quarterly Segment Revenues And Segment Income


CHARLOTTE, N.C. – October 29, 2026 – Sonic Automotive, Inc. (“Sonic Automotive,” “Sonic,” the “Company,” “we,” “us” or “our”) (NYSE:SAH), one of the nation’s largest automotive retailers, today reported financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Summary
•All-time record quarterly total revenues of $4.0 billion, up 0.4% year-over-year; all-time quarterly record total gross profit of $617.5 million, up 0.3% year-over-year
•Reported net income in the third quarter was $41.1 million, down 12% year-over-year ($1.28 earnings per diluted share, down 4% year-over-year)
▪Adjusted net income* in the third quarter was $41.9 million, down 15% year-over-year ($1.30 adjusted earnings per diluted share*, down 8% year-over-year)
•Total reported selling, general and administrative (“SG&A”) expenses as a percentage of gross profit of 75.6% (75.7% on a Franchised Dealerships Segment basis, 82.1% on an EchoPark Segment basis, and 60.7% on a Powersports Segment basis)
▪Total adjusted SG&A expenses as a percentage of gross profit* of 75.4% (75.6% on a Franchised Dealerships Segment basis, 81.1% on an EchoPark Segment basis, and 60.7% on a Powersports Segment basis)
•EchoPark Segment revenues of $596.0 million, up 14% year-over-year; third quarter record EchoPark Segment total gross profit of $58.9 million, up 8% year-over-year; EchoPark Segment retail used vehicle unit sales volume of 19,923, up 22% year-over-year
•Reported EchoPark Segment income of $2.0 million, as compared to $2.6 million in the prior year period, a 23% decrease year-over-year
▪Adjusted EchoPark Segment income* of $2.6 million, as compared to $2.7 million in the prior year period, a 4% decrease year-over-year
•EchoPark Segment adjusted EBITDA* of $7.9 million, as compared to $8.2 million in the prior year period, down 4% year-over-year
•Sonic’s Board of Directors approved a quarterly cash dividend of $0.41 per share, payable on January 15, 2027 to all stockholders of record on December 15, 2026



* Represents a non-GAAP financial measure — please refer to the discussion and reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures below.

Commentary
David Smith, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of Sonic Automotive, stated, “Our third quarter results reflect the value of Sonic’s diversified business, as revenue and gross profit growth in our EchoPark and Powersports segments offset declines in our Franchised Dealerships segment, driving all-time record quarterly consolidated revenues and gross profit. We remain confident in the long-term opportunities across our businesses and continue to focus on providing a world-class guest experience, which we believe will support our long-term strategic investments in order to deliver sustainable earnings growth and stockholder value.”

Jeff Dyke, President of Sonic Automotive, commented, “Within our Franchised Dealerships segment, we continue to focus on strengthening performance across our core operations, emphasizing expense leverage opportunities while navigating a challenging consumer affordability environment. EchoPark remains a significant strategic growth opportunity for Sonic — building on 22% used vehicle unit volume growth in the third quarter, we are excited to announce our new EchoPark brand campaign and Orlando market opening in the coming weeks. I'd also like to congratulate our entire Powersports team on another record-setting Sturgis Motorcycle Rally and the seamless integration of our recent acquisitions into the Sonic Powersports family, driving record-setting quarterly performance in this segment.”

Heath Byrd, Chief Financial Officer of Sonic Automotive, added, “We are taking action to prioritize improved expense leverage in the near term, while maintaining certain strategic investments that we expect to drive future profitability and operational efficiency across our business. We remain focused on disciplined capital allocation, evaluating growth investments and capital returns against expected returns and liquidity needs.”

Third Quarter 2026 Segment Highlights
The financial measures discussed below are results for the third quarter of 2026 with comparisons made to the third quarter of 2025, unless otherwise noted.
•Franchised Dealerships Segment operating results include:
•Same store revenues down 2%; same store gross profit down 1%
•Same store retail new vehicle unit sales volume down 7%; same store retail new vehicle gross profit per unit was flat, at $3,000 per unit
•Same store retail used vehicle unit sales volume up 3%; same store retail used vehicle gross profit per unit down 3%, to $1,506 per unit
•Same store parts, service and collision repair (“Fixed Operations”) gross profit up 1%; same store customer pay gross profit was flat; same store warranty gross profit up 1%; same store Fixed Operations gross profit margin up 20 basis points, to 51.4%
•Same store finance and insurance (“F&I”) gross profit was flat; same store F&I gross profit per retail unit of $2,630, up 2%
•On a trailing quarter cost of sales basis, the Franchised Dealerships Segment had 65 days’ supply of new vehicle inventory (including in-transit) and 34 days’ supply of used vehicle inventory


•EchoPark Segment operating results include:
•Revenues of $596.0 million, up 14%; third quarter record gross profit of $58.9 million, up 8%
•Retail used vehicle unit sales volume of 19,923, up 22%; total combined gross profit per unit of $2,972, down 12%
•Quarterly reported segment income of $2.0 million, quarterly adjusted segment income* of $2.6 million, and quarterly adjusted EBITDA* of $7.9 million
•On a trailing quarter cost of sales basis, the EchoPark Segment had 41 days’ supply of used vehicle inventory
•Powersports Segment operating results include:
•All-time record quarterly revenues of $113.7 million, up 35%; all-time record quarterly gross profit of $29.3 million, up 26%
•Segment income of $7.9 million, up 1%, and adjusted EBITDA* of $10.5 million, up 4%

* Represents a non-GAAP financial measure — please refer to the discussion and reconciliation of non-GAAP financial measures below.

About Sonic Automotive
For more than 60 years, Sonic Automotive has pursued a single purpose: to deliver an experience for our guests and our teammates that fulfills dreams, enriches lives, and delivers happiness. We don’t simply sell and service vehicles. We help people pursue their dreams, whether it’s a guest purchasing their first vehicle, a family creating lifelong memories, or a teammate building a meaningful career.

Founded in 1966 by Bruton Smith, the company has grown into a Fortune 300 company under the leadership of Chairman and CEO David B. Smith. Today, more than 11,000 teammates bring the company’s purpose to life across a nationwide network of 174 automotive and powersports franchises in 146 locations in 90 cities and 21 states. We are proud to represent 24 automotive and 15 powersports brands and have helped more than 7 million guests purchase vehicles, delivered over 40 million service experiences, and earned more than 1 million 5-star reviews by consistently putting people first.



At Sonic Automotive, we believe trust isn’t claimed – it’s earned through transparency, consistency, integrity, and genuine care. That’s why we were the only automotive and powersports retailer recognized by Newsweek as one of America’s Most Trustworthy Companies in 2026. As the automotive and powersports industries continue to evolve, our mission remains constant: to innovate, lead with integrity, and create exceptional experiences that inspire confidence, build lifelong relationships, and positively impact every life we touch.
