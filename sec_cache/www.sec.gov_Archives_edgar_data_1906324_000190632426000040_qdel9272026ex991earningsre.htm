QuidelOrtho Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results
― Growth in Labs and Immunohematology Outside of China Highlights Underlying Business Strength ―
― New Cost Transformation and Enterprise Restructuring Program Expected to Deliver Over $80 Million in Annual Savings in 2027 ―
― Updated Full-Year 2026 Financial Guidance Narrowing Prior Ranges ―


November 3, 2026

SAN DIEGO, CA November 3, 2026 — QuidelOrtho Corporation (Nasdaq: QDEL) (the “Company” or “QuidelOrtho”), a leading global provider of diagnostic solutions, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 27, 2026.

Key Third Quarter 2026 Results:
(all comparisons are to the prior year period)

•Total revenue was $619 million, a decrease of 12% both as reported and in constant currency. Excluding respiratory, China, and the U.S. Donor Screening business, total revenue grew 2% as reported and 3% in constant currency, reflecting the underlying strength of the business.
◦Labs revenue of $353 million declined 6% both as reported and in constant currency, primarily due to lower sales in China related to recently announced changes to In Vitro Diagnostics pricing. Outside China, Labs revenue grew 3% as reported and 4% in constant currency, driven by continued strength across the business.
◦Immunohematology revenue of $142 million was flat as reported and increased 1% in constant currency. Outside China, Immunohematology revenue grew 2% as reported and 3% in constant currency.
◦Point of Care revenue of $120 million declined 27% both as reported and in constant currency due to a softer respiratory season.
•GAAP net loss was $54 million; GAAP operating loss was $8 million; adjusted EBITDA was $148 million.
•GAAP net loss margin was 8.7%; GAAP operating loss margin was 1.2%; adjusted EBITDA margin was 23.9%.
•GAAP diluted loss per share was $0.78; adjusted diluted earnings per share (“EPS”) was $0.31.
•Operating cash flow was $20 million; free cash flow was $0.3 million.

“The underlying performance of our Labs and Immunohematology businesses remained solid in the third quarter, supported by the strength and durability of our customer relationships and our large global installed base,” said Brian J. Blaser, President and Chief Executive Officer of QuidelOrtho. “While China and a softer respiratory season continue to pressure our results, we are taking decisive action to strengthen the Company through a new cost transformation and enterprise restructuring program. This program is designed to improve profitability and cash flow while evolving our operating model around the distinct needs of our customers and markets. We believe these actions will help mitigate near-term headwinds and position the Company for sustainable long-term growth.” 

Cost Transformation and Restructuring Program

The Company has initiated a new cost transformation and enterprise restructuring program designed to further improve its cost structure and evolve its operating model around two focused businesses, Core Diagnostics and Point of Care Diagnostics. The new operating model is designed to increase customer focus, create clearer accountability for business performance, and better align technology development and investment priorities with the distinct needs of each business. The Company expects the program to deliver over $80 million in annual savings in 2027 and to record approximately $30 to $35 million in one-time restructuring charges in the fourth quarter of 2026.

About QuidelOrtho Corporation

With expertise spanning clinical chemistry, immunoassay, immunohematology and molecular testing, QuidelOrtho Corporation (Nasdaq: QDEL) is a leading global provider of diagnostic solutions, dedicated to advancing fast, accurate and reliable results that help improve patient outcomes – from the point of care to hospital, lab to clinic. Building on a legacy of innovation, QuidelOrtho works with healthcare providers to advance diagnostics that connect insights with solutions, defining a clearer path for informed decisions and better care.
