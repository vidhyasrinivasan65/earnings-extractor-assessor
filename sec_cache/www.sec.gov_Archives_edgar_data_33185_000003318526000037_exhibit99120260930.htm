Equifax Delivers Strong 10% Revenue Growth in Third Quarter 2026 Driven by 8% Diversified Markets Revenue Growth Despite Challenging U.S. Mortgage Market; Returns $158 Million Cash to Shareholders

ATLANTA, OCTOBER 20, 2026 -- Equifax® (NYSE: EFX) today announced financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.

•Third quarter reported revenue of $1.693 billion, up a strong 10% with 9% local currency revenue growth.
•Diversified Markets revenue up 8% on a reported and 7.5% on a local currency basis, with strong, broad-based performances across all business units.
•Third quarter U.S. Mortgage revenue up 15% despite weaker market.
•Workforce Solutions third quarter revenue up 7%. Diversified Markets revenue up 9% led by Verification Services Diversified Markets revenue up 13% with Talent Solutions revenue growth over 20%, Consumer Lending up high double digits, and Government up mid-single digits. Workforce Solutions Mortgage revenue flat in the third quarter from the weaker mortgage market.
•USIS third quarter revenue up strong 13% with Diversified Markets revenue growth up 6% led by Financial Institutions and Auto. USIS Mortgage revenue up a strong 28% despite the weaker mortgage market.
•International third quarter revenue up 9% on a reported basis. Local currency revenue growth up 7% with low double-digit revenue growth in Australia and high single-digit growth in Latin America. Expect Círculo de Crédito acquisition to close in the fourth quarter.
•New Product Innovation leveraging the EFX Cloud, EFX.AI, and proprietary data delivered record over 17% new product Vitality Index.
•Returned $158 million in cash to shareholders through share repurchases and quarterly dividend.

“Equifax delivered a strong third quarter performance executing on our EFX2028 Strategic Priorities with reported revenue of $1.693 billion, up 10% on a reported basis, with 9% local currency revenue growth. Diversified Markets revenue growth of 8% was broad based across all business units driven by a record over 17% new product Vitality Index, above our 10% long-term goal. U.S. Mortgage revenue grew 15%, despite the much weaker mortgage market we saw in the latter half of the third quarter as mortgage interest rates increased substantially.

Workforce Solutions delivered 7% revenue growth, with Diversified Markets growth of 9% led by strong over 20% growth in Talent Solutions, strong high double-digit growth in Consumer Lending and accelerating sequential mid single-digit growth in Government. Workforce Solutions Mortgage revenue was about flat from the weak mortgage market. USIS delivered strong revenue growth of 13%, with Diversified Markets revenue growth of 6%, led by Financial Institutions and Auto. USIS Mortgage revenue was up 28% despite the weaker mortgage market. International delivered 7% local currency revenue growth with low double-digit revenue growth in Australia and high single-digit growth in Latin America.

We continue to deploy EFX.AI to deliver new, higher-performing products and solutions to our customers and expand AI deployment inside EFX to deliver against our $150 million AI productivity goal. With our strong free cash flow, Equifax returned $158 million of cash to shareholders in the quarter, including repurchasing 0.5 million shares for $92 million and paying $66 million

“As we look to the remainder of 2026, we are maintaining our Diversified Markets revenue guidance for revenue growth of high single-digit percent. However, we expect the much weaker U.S. mortgage market that we saw in the latter half of the third quarter to continue through the remainder of 2026. As a result, we are reducing our full year 2026 revenue guidance at the midpoint to $6.661 billion, reflecting this reduction in our U.S. Mortgage related revenue.

Despite the impact of the significantly weakened U.S. mortgage market on the second half of 2026 revenue, we expect to deliver 2026 Adjusted EPS Guidance of $8.50 per share, a reduction of only $0.04 per share at the midpoint and well within our previous Guidance range. As we did in the second quarter, the Equifax team is doing an outstanding job of managing cost and expenses including continuing to drive strong AI-driven operating cost efficiencies and optimizations, which should allow us to principally recover the gross margin impact from lower Mortgage revenue and deliver very strong operating margin performance.

We are confident in the future of the New Equifax. Equifax is fundamentally a different company on how we go to market from Technology to Data & Analytics, EFX.AI capabilities, product focus, and AI-driven Operations all leveraging our differentiated data assets, industry-leading Cloud technology, patented EFX.AI products, D&A capabilities, and market-leading innovation. Our scale proprietary data is the foundation of our AI data moat and a big competitive advantage, and we are expanding our capabilities to leverage our unique, non-public data assets with EFX.AI and agentic AI to rapidly deliver higher-performing scores, models, and multi-market products to help our customers grow.

Our strong third quarter results reflect the resiliency of the broad-based Equifax business model in a challenging mortgage market. We are energized about the New Equifax and we expect to deliver higher growth, margins, and accelerating free cash flow, and returning cash to shareholders in the future."

Financial Results Summary

The Company reported revenue of $1,692.5 million in the third quarter of 2026, up 10 percent and 9 percent on a reported and local currency basis, respectively, compared to the third quarter of 2025.

Net income attributable to Equifax of $211.7 million was up 32 percent in the third quarter of 2026 compared to $160.2 million in the third quarter of 2025.

Diluted EPS attributable to Equifax was $1.79 per share in the third quarter of 2026, up 39 percent compared to $1.29 per share in the third quarter of 2025.

Workforce Solutions Third Quarter Results

•Total revenue was $693.9 million in the third quarter of 2026, up 7 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025. Operating margin for Workforce Solutions was 44.1 percent in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 43.8 percent in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA margin for Workforce Solutions was 51.4 percent in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 51.2 percent in the third quarter of 2025.
•Verification Services revenue was $603.2 million, up 9 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Employer Services revenue was $90.7 million, down 5 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025.

USIS Third Quarter Results

•Total revenue was $599.1 million in the third quarter of 2026, up 13 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025. Operating margin for USIS was 24.3 percent in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 23.2 percent in the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted EBITDA margin for USIS was 35.4 percent in the third quarter of 2026 compared to 35.2 percent in the third quarter of 2025.
•Online Information Solutions revenue was $529.7 million, up 13 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025.
•Financial Marketing Services revenue was $69.4 million, up 11 percent compared to the third quarter of 2025.

About Equifax

At Equifax (NYSE: EFX), we believe knowledge drives progress. Notes to Reconciliations of Non-GAAP Financial Measures to the Comparable GAAP Financial Measures

Diluted EPS attributable to Equifax is adjusted for the following items:

Acquisition-related amortization expense - During the third quarter of 2026 and 2025, we recorded acquisition-related amortization expense of certain acquired intangibles of $59.9 million ($48.0 million, net of tax) and $62.7 million ($50.2 million, net of tax), respectively. We calculate this financial measure by excluding the impact of acquisition-related amortization expense and including a benefit to reflect the material cash income tax savings resulting from the income tax deductibility of amortization for certain acquired intangibles. These financial measures are not prepared in conformity with GAAP. Management believes excluding the impact of amortization expense is useful because excluding acquisition-related amortization, and other items that are not comparable, allows investors to evaluate our performance for different periods on a more comparable basis. Certain acquired intangibles result in material cash income tax savings which are not reflected in earnings. Management believes that including a benefit to reflect the cash income tax savings is useful as it allows investors to better value Equifax. Management makes these adjustments to earnings when measuring profitability, evaluating performance trends, setting performance objectives and calculating our return on invested capital.
