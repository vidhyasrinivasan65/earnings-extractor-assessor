Cardiff Oncology Reports Third Quarter 2026 Results and Provides Business Update

 

Phase 2 trial remains ongoing, with median PFS not reached in the 30 mg onvansertib + FOLFIRI/bev arm versus median PFS of approximately 11 months in the combined SoC arms

 

 

All patients remaining in the 30 mg onvansertib + FOLFIRI/bev arm have now exceeded 21 months on treatment

 

 

Company preparing for registrational Phase 3 trial in first-line RAS-mutated mCRC, with potential start in Q1 2027

 

SAN DIEGO, Calif., November 12, 2026 — Cardiff Oncology, Inc. (Nasdaq: CRDF), a clinical-stage biotechnology company targeting RAS-mutated cancer by leveraging PLK1 inhibition, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026, and provided a business update.

“We’re greatly encouraged by the performance of onvansertib in first-line RAS-mutated mCRC. As the data mature, the benefit of onvansertib in combination with FOLFIRI/bev is becoming more evident, with growing separation between the onvansertib and control arms,” said Mani Mohindru, PhD, President and Chief Executive Officer of Cardiff Oncology. “Building on the strength of these data, we are actively preparing for our upcoming registrational trial. There are currently no treatment options specifically approved for patients with pan RAS-mutated mCRC in the first-line setting and we believe onvansertib has the potential to become an important new treatment for these patients.”

Clinical Highlights

 

Randomized Phase 2 Trial in First-Line RAS-Mutated Metastatic Colorectal Cancer (“mCRC”) Ongoing

 

Cardiff’s randomized Phase 2 trial evaluating onvansertib in combination with FOLFIRI/ bevacizumab (“bev”) or FOLFOX/bev as first-line therapy in patients with RAS-mutated mCRC remains ongoing. Median progression-free survival (“PFS”) has not been reached in the 30 mg onvansertib + FOLFIRI/bev arm, the Company’s proposed Phase 3 regimen.

 

As of September 28, 2026, 6 patients remain on the FOLFIRI/bev regimen, with 5 patients in the onvansertib (20 or 30 mg) + FOLFIRI/bev arms and 1 patient on standard-of-care (“SoC”) FOLFIRI/bev. Patients who remain in the 30 mg onvansertib + FOLFIRI/bev arm have been on treatment for at least 21 months, with the longest duration of treatment being over 29 months.

 

For comparison, the median PFS in the combined SoC arms has been reached at approximately 11 months, consistent with what has been historically reported.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

 

Liquidity, cash burn, and cash runway

 

As of September 30, 2026, Cardiff Oncology had approximately $33.8 million in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments. Net cash used in operating activities for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was approximately $33.9 million, an increase of $2.0 million from $31.9 million for the same period in 2025. In July 2026, the Company raised net proceeds of approximately $9.1 million in a registered direct offering. Based on its current expectations and projections, the Company believes its current cash resources are sufficient to fund its operations to the end of the third quarter of 2027.

 

Operating results

 

Total operating expenses were approximately $32.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, a decrease of $9.5 million from $41.5 million for the same period in 2025. R&D expenses decreased by $12.5 million, reflecting the completion of clinical trials, fewer patients on treatment in the Phase 2 mCRC trial, a reduction in preclinical activities, and completion of chemistry, manufacturing and controls (“CMC”) activities as the Company prepares for its planned Phase 3 mCRC trial. SG&A expenses increased by $3.1 million, driven by legal and professional fees related to the Company’s dispute with NMS, which was resolved on September 14, 2026, and also by employee severance costs. Net loss was $30.4 million, compared with $38.6 million for the prior year period.

 

About Cardiff Oncology, Inc.

 

 

 

Cardiff Oncology is a clinical-stage biotechnology company targeting RAS-mutated cancers via innovative cancer treatments focused on PLK1 inhibition, a validated oncology target with practice-changing potential. Our lead asset, onvansertib, is a highly specific, oral PLK1 inhibitor currently being evaluated in a Phase 2 trial for first-line treatment of RAS-mutated metastatic colorectal cancer (“mCRC”), addressing a large, underserved patient population with high unmet need. Onvansertib is also under investigation in other PLK1-driven cancers through ongoing investigator-initiated trials and has shown robust single-agent clinical activity in hard-to-treat tumors. By targeting tumor vulnerabilities, we aim to overcome treatment resistance and deliver improved clinical outcomes for patients.

For more information, please visit https://www.cardiffoncology.com.
