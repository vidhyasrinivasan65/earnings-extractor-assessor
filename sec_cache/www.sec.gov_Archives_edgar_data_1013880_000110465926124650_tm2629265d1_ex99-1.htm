TTEC Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and

Reaffirms Outlook for Full Year 2026

 

 

AUSTIN, Texas, November 9, 2026 – TTEC Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ:TTEC), a leading global technology, consulting and managed services company focused on delivering solutions at the intersection of data, AI and customer experience, announced today financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

"Our third-quarter results show that the specific plans we laid out for this year are taking hold. Across TTEC Engage and TTEC Digital, we executed against our commitments; improving sales pipeline quality, strengthening program economics, expanding margins, and putting purpose-built AI tools into the hands of our frontline teams to create better outcomes for our clients and their customers," commented Ken Tuchman, TTEC chairman and chief executive officer.

 

Tuchman continued, "We recognize that there is more work ahead to return to our historical levels of growth and profitability, but the progress is measurable and improving. Our clients have said that they need a partner who can bring AI and human expertise together securely and at scale, and that is what we are delivering. With a healthy backlog and a team focused on execution, we enter the fourth quarter with momentum and confidence in our direction.”

 

THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL HIGHLIGHTS

Revenue

·Third quarter 2026 GAAP revenue was $492.2 million, a 5.2 percent decrease compared to $519.1 million in the prior year.

·Foreign exchange had a $2.1 million negative impact on revenue in the third quarter of 2026.

 

Income from Operations

·Third quarter 2026 GAAP income from operations was $23.3 million, or 4.7 percent of revenue, compared to $12.3 million, or 2.4 percent of revenue in the prior year.
·Non-GAAP income from operations, excluding restructuring and impairment charges, equity-based compensation expenses, amortization of purchased intangibles, and other items, was $37.3 million, or 7.6 percent of revenue, compared to $28.9 million, or 5.6 percent of revenue in the prior year.

·Foreign exchange had a $1.5 million positive impact on Non-GAAP income from operations in the third quarter of 2026.

 

Adjusted EBITDA

·Third quarter 2026 Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA was $51.5 million, or 10.5 percent of revenue, compared to $43.4 million, or 8.4 percent of revenue in the prior year.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

Earnings Per Share

·Third quarter 2026 GAAP fully diluted net loss per share was $0.06 compared to net loss per share of $0.23 in the prior year.

·Non-GAAP fully diluted earnings per share was $0.22 compared to $0.12 in the prior year.

 

 

CASH FLOW AND BALANCE SHEET

 

·Cash flow from operations in the third quarter of 2026 was $37.0 million compared to $4.2 million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

·Free cash flow in the third quarter of 2026 was a positive $27.9 million compared to negative $9.6 million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

·Capital expenditures in the third quarter of 2026 were $9.1 million compared to $13.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.

 

·As of September 30, 2026, TTEC had cash and cash equivalents of $91.1 million and debt of $834.7 million, resulting in a net debt position of $743.6 million. This compares to a net debt position of $812.5 million for the same period in 2025.

 

·On November 9, 2026, TTEC extended the term of its Credit Facility to November 2028. For further details about our Credit Facility and its terms, please refer to our disclosure in our quarterly report on Form 10-Q.

 

 

SEGMENT REPORTING & COMMENTARY

 

TTEC reports financial results for TTEC Digital and TTEC Engage business segments. Financial highlights for the two business segments are provided below.

 

TTEC Digital

·Third quarter 2026 GAAP revenue for TTEC Digital was $122.1 million, an increase of 0.2 percent compared to $121.9 million for the year ago period.
·Income from operations was $6.7 million or 5.5 percent of revenue compared to $4.9 million or 4.0 percent of revenue in the prior year.
·Non-GAAP income from operations was $12.5 million, or 10.3 percent of revenue compared to operating income of $11.6 million or 9.5 percent of revenue in the prior year.

 

TTEC Engage

·Third quarter 2026 GAAP revenue for TTEC Engage was $370.1 million, a 6.8 percent decrease from $397.2 million for the year ago period.
·Income from operations was $16.6 million or 4.5 percent of revenue compared to $7.5 million or 1.9 percent of revenue in the prior year.

 

 

 

 

 

 

 

·Non-GAAP income from operations was $24.8 million, or 6.7 percent of revenue, compared to operating income of $17.3 million, or 4.3 percent of revenue in the prior year.
·Foreign exchange had a $2.1 million negative impact on revenue and a $1.4 million positive impact on income from operations.

 

 

BUSINESS OUTLOOK

 

“Our third quarter financial results delivered strong profitable growth across both segments, reflecting our disciplined execution of our 2026 priorities. In Engage, our continued focus on right shoring delivery, rationalizing and improving underperforming client programs, managing costs and driving operational efficiencies is resulting in margin improvement. Our Digital segment continues to perform in line with expectations with solid double-digit expansion in our growth practices. As a result, we expect the fourth quarter to continue to reflect higher margins both sequentially and year-over-year for both segments,” commented Kenny Wagers, chief financial officer of TTEC.

 

Wagers continued, “The third quarter also delivered solid cash flow as we continue to deleverage our balance sheet. Year to date, we have generated approximately $88 million of free cash flow and reduced our net debt by over $81 million, ending in a net debt position of $744 million. We successfully amended our credit facility, extending the maturity date to the fourth quarter of 2028, providing us flexibility to continue to execute on our strategic priorities.”

ABOUT TTEC

 

TTEC (pronounced T-TEC) Holdings, Inc. (NASDAQ:TTEC) is a leading global CX (customer experience) technology and services innovator for AI-enabled digital CX solutions. Serving iconic and disruptive brands, TTEC's outcome-based solutions span the entire enterprise, touch every virtual interaction channel, and improve each step of the customer journey. Leveraging next-gen digital technology, the Company's TTEC Digital business designs, builds, and operates omnichannel contact center technology, CRM, AI and analytics solutions. The company's TTEC Engage business delivers AI-enabled customer engagement, customer acquisition and growth, tech support, back office, and fraud prevention services. Founded in 1982, the company's singular obsession with CX excellence has earned it leading client, customer, and employee satisfaction scores across the globe. The company's employees operate on six continents and bring technology and humanity together to deliver happy customers and differentiated business results. To learn more visit us at https://www.ttec.com.
