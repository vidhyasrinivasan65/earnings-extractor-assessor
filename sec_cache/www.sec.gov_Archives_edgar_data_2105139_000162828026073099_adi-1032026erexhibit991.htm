ADI Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Updates Full-Year Outlook
•Net revenue of $1,306 million, up 9% year-over-year; average daily sales increased 5%
•Gross margin of 20.9%; down 170 basis points year-over-year
•Net loss of $655 million, including a $659 million non-cash goodwill impairment charge, compared to net income
of $26 million in the prior-year quarter; Adjusted Standalone EBITDA1 of $66 million, or 5.1% of net revenue,
compared to $76 million or 6.3% of net revenue in the prior-year quarter
•Net cash provided by operating activities of $64 million
•Updates full-year 2026 financial guidance
MELVILLE, New York, November 12, 2026 – ADI Global Distribution Inc. (“ADI”) (NYSE: ADIG), a leading global
specialty distributor of low-voltage products, today announced financial results for its third quarter 2026 ended October 3,
2026 and updated its outlook for the remainder of 2026.
ADI operated as part of Resideo Technologies, Inc. (“Resideo”) for a portion of the third quarter of 2026 prior to the spin-
off on August 3, 2026, and the historical financial information presented in this release prior to the spin-off was derived
from Resideo’s accounting records and is presented on a carve-out accounting basis.
ADI third quarter 2026 net revenue was $1,306 million, up 8.6% year-over-year, reflecting average daily sales growth of
5% year-over-year and two additional selling days in the current quarter. Growth also benefited from an easier comparison
with the prior-year quarter, when revenue was affected by disruptions associated with the Americas ERP conversion.
Higher sales in security, professional audio-visual, fire and life safety and data communications were partially offset by
lower residential audio-visual sales, primarily reflecting continued weakness in the U.S. housing market.
Gross margin was 20.9% in third quarter 2026, compared to 22.6% in third quarter 2025. The decline primarily relates to
unfavorable customer and product mix and inventory-related charges, as well as the comparison with a temporary prior-
year tariff benefit when selling-price increases took effect ahead of higher inventory costs. These pressures were partially
offset by tariff refunds and a benefit from spin-off related inventory adjustments. Excluding these two items, gross margin
was 20.0%.
Research and development expenses decreased $1 million due primarily to people and operating expense reductions.
Selling, general and administrative expenses were up $12 million to $202 million primarily reflecting higher facility costs
associated with real estate and distribution center consolidation, merit and cost inflation impacts, investments in third-party
spend in the business, increases in finance, legal, human resources, information technology, and other standalone costs
which were higher as compared to those allocated in the carve-out financials in the prior period.
During the third quarter of 2026, the Company recognized a non-cash goodwill impairment charge of $659 million
following an interim quantitative impairment assessment that was triggered by continued weakness in the residential
market, together with lower projected earnings and cash flows, declines in our stock price and market capitalization. The
revised projections reflect continued residential market weakness affecting demand for Exclusive Brands products and
unfavorable product and customer mix.
Including this charge, loss from operations was $634 million in third quarter 2026 compared to income from operations of
$41 million in third quarter 2025.
Adjusted Standalone EBITDA decreased 13% to $66 million in third quarter 2026 compared to $76 million in third quarter
2025.
Management Remarks
“Third-quarter profitability was below our expectations, primarily reflecting a continued challenging residential market and
weaker-than-anticipated demand and product mix in our Exclusive Brands offering. A greater share of commercial sales
from lower-margin national accounts and projects also weighed on profitability. These results fell short of our standards,
and we are lowering our full-year outlook to reflect the third-quarter shortfall and further weakness in Exclusive Brands in
the fourth quarter,” said Rob Aarnes, ADI’s President and CEO.
“With our transition to an independent public company complete, we are focused on strengthening execution and
improving performance in a challenging operating environment. We expect to exit 2026 with approximately $60 million in
annualized gross cost savings and remain on track to achieve our $80 million objective by the end of 2027 on a run rate
basis. We are also developing additional cost reductions beyond that program to align our cost structure with current
demand trends. In parallel, we are executing our One ADI transformation, integrating our systems and simplifying our
operations to support long-term growth. These efforts, together with our leading market positions and differentiated
omnichannel model, position ADI for long-term profitable growth and shareholder value creation.”
Balance Sheet and Capital Allocation
As of October 3, 2026, ADI's liquidity consists of approximately $208 million of cash and cash equivalents and a $500
million fully undrawn revolving credit facility. ADI expects to generate consistent cash flow, benefiting from its capital-
efficient business model.
Outlook
The Company is lowering its full-year 2026 outlook to reflect third-quarter performance and further weakness in Exclusive
Brands in October. The revised outlook assumes these demand and mix pressures persist through the remainder of the year.

About ADI
ADI is a global specialty distributor of professionally installed low-voltage products serving commercial and residential
markets through an omnichannel go-to-market platform. Within North America, ADI is a market-leading distributor in the
professionally installed security, fire/life safety and residential audio-visual product categories. We offer over 500,000
products from more than 1,000 suppliers across key specialty low-voltage categories with strong proximity to our
customers with a large network of store locations.

Non-GAAP Financial Measures
This press release includes certain “non-GAAP financial measures” as defined under the Securities Exchange Act of 1934.
Management believes the use of such non-GAAP financial measures assists investors in understanding the ongoing
operating performance of ADI by presenting financial results between periods on a more comparable basis. Such non-
GAAP financial measures should not be construed as an alternative to reported results determined in accordance with U.S.
GAAP. Readers should also consider the limitations associated with these non-GAAP financial measures, including the
potential lack of comparability of these measures from one company to another.
The Company uses non-GAAP financial measures to supplement the financial measures prepared in accordance with U.S.
GAAP. These include Adjusted EBITDA, Adjusted EBITDA Margin, Adjusted Standalone EBITDA (estimate) and
Adjusted Standalone EBITDA margin (estimate).
Below are definitions and reconciliations of certain non-GAAP financial measures to the most directly comparable
financial measures calculated and presented in accordance with U.S. GAAP. Management believes that, when considered
together with reported amounts, these measures are useful to investors and management in understanding our ongoing
operations and in the analysis of ongoing operating trends. Management believes these non-GAAP financial measures
provide investors with a meaningful measure of its performance period to period, align the measures to how management
evaluates performance internally, and make it easier for investors to compare our performance to peers. These measures
should be considered in addition to, and not as replacements for, the most directly comparable U.S. GAAP measure.
The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA as net (loss) income excluding income taxes, depreciation and amortization,
interest income and expense, stock-based compensation expense, Indemnification Agreement expense, restructuring
expense, transaction related expenses, and other expense and certain other items that are otherwise of an unusual or non-
recurring nature (including but not limited to impairment charges, litigation and insurance settlements, and gains and losses
on disposal of assets). The Company defines Adjusted EBITDA Margin as Adjusted EBITDA divided by Net revenue.
The Company defines Adjusted Standalone EBITDA (estimate) as Adjusted EBITDA less estimated recurring and ongoing
costs required to operate as a new independent public company. The Company defines Adjusted Standalone EBITDA
Margin (estimate) as Adjusted Standalone EBITDA (estimate) divided by Net revenue.
We believe these measures are useful to investors as they provide greater transparency with respect to supplemental
information used by management in its financial and operational decision making, as well as understanding ongoing
operating trends.
A reconciliation of the forecasted range for Adjusted Standalone EBITDA (estimate) for the fourth quarter of 2026 and for
the full year 2026 are not included in this release due to the number of variables in the projected range and because we are
currently unable to quantify accurately without unreasonable efforts certain amounts that would be required to be included
in the U.S. GAAP measure or the individual adjustments for such reconciliation. In addition, we believe such reconciliation
would imply a degree of precision that would be confusing or misleading to investors.
