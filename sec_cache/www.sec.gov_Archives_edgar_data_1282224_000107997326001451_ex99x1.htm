 Dolphin Entertainment Reports Record Revenue in Third Quarter 2026

 

Q3’26 Revenue Rises 8.3% YoY to a record $16.0 Million; Net Income of $0.2 Million or $0.02 per share; Adjusted EBITDA Up 42.9% YoY to $1.6 Million or $0.12 per share

 

Anticipates Four Ventures in 2027, with First Expected in Q1 2027

 

MIAMI, FL / ACCESS Newswire / November 10, 2026 / Dolphin (NASDAQ:DLPN), a leading entertainment marketing and premium content production company, today announced its financial results for the third quarter ended September 30, 2026.

 

Bill O’Dowd, CEO of Dolphin, commented:

 

“Dolphin had an outstanding third quarter. Revenue for the quarter grew 8.3% year-over-year to $16.0 million, the highest quarterly revenue in Dolphin’s history, even including Q1 2024 when we reported $3.4 million from Blue Angels. This is all organic growth, and actually would have been 9.7% excluding Always Alpha, which we spun-out in November 2025. For the first nine months, revenue grew 5.4% to $43.3 million, or 6.5% excluding Always Alpha.

 

Last quarter, we said to expect a meaningful step up in profitability in the third quarter, and that is what we delivered. We reported net income of $0.2 million, compared to a net loss of $0.4 million a year ago, and adjusted EBITDA increased 42.9% to $1.6 million versus $1.1 million a year ago. For the first nine months, adjusted EBITDA increased 24% to $1.4 million versus $1.1 million a year ago. We believe that our large non-cash amortization expenses often obscure our cash flow results, which is why we prefer to measure ourselves using adjusted EBITDA versus net income. We built Dolphin to grow steadily and to grow profits faster than revenue, and this quarter shows it. Revenue grew more than 8%, well ahead of growth in our core operating costs.

 

We expect continued improved operating results in the quarters ahead, especially as we get stronger each quarter in cross-selling services between our various agencies. We expect to continue to be able to leverage our horizontal scale across the major sectors of entertainment, and our vertical scale in the various disciplines of earned media.

 

The free cash flow drivers we have discussed also remain on schedule. The largest of our New York and Los Angeles leases expire in the second half of 2027, from which we expect to save more than $1 million a year, and our primary bank term loan matures in September 2028, which will free up approximately $2.2 million a year in principal and interest payments. With approximately $127 million in NOL carryforwards, we expect most of these savings to flow directly to free cash flow.

 

We also expect 2027 to be a big year for our ventures. We have identified our next two ventures and expect at least one to be in market in the first quarter of 2027, and we are on track for three more during the year, for a total of four in 2027.

 

At Graviteur Studios, Kristen Brancaccio will direct our first feature, “Crush Party.” Kristen directed Issa Rae’s vertical series Screen Time, the most-watched English-language microdrama on TikTok, with more than 580 million views. Casting is underway, and production is slated to begin in the first half of 2027.

 

Finally, I have continued to buy Dolphin stock every week under my 10b5-1 plan and now own over 5% of the common shares outstanding. Management remains closely aligned with shareholders in building long-term value.”

 

Q3 2026 and Recent Highlights

 

•Total revenue for the three months ended September 30, 2026, was $16.0 million, the highest quarterly revenue in the Company’s history and an increase of 8.3% from $14.8 million last year. Total revenue for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was $43.3 million, an increase of 5.4% from $41.1 million last year.

 

•Income from operations was $0.8 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, an increase of 167% from $0.3 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025.

 

•Operating expenses for Q3 2026 were $15.2 million, including non-cash depreciation and amortization of $0.5 million and approximately $0.4 million of litigation-related legal costs. This compares to operating expenses of $14.5 million in Q3 2025.

 

•Net income for Q3 2026 was $0.2 million, compared to a net loss of $0.4 million for Q3 2025.

 

•Basic and diluted earnings per share for Q3 2026 was $0.02 based on 13,029,555 and 13,093,494 weighted average shares outstanding, respectively, compared to basic and diluted loss per share in Q3 2025 of $(0.03) based on 11,770,195 weighted average shares outstanding.

 

 
 

 

•Adjusted EBITDA for Q3 2026 was approximately $1.6 million, an increase of 42.9% from approximately $1.1 million in Q3 2025. Adjusted EBITDA basic earnings per share for Q3 2026 was $0.12 based on 13,029,555 weighted average shares outstanding, and fully diluted earnings per share was $0.08 based on 20,090,590 weighted average shares outstanding, compared to Adjusted EBITDA for Q3 2025 of $1.1 million, or $0.09 basic earnings per share and $0.06 fully diluted earnings per share based on 11,770,195 and 17,842,349, respectively, weighted average shares outstanding.

 

•Adjusted EBITDA for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, was approximately $1.4 million, an increase of 24.2% from approximately $1.1 million for the same period last year.

 

•Cash and cash equivalents were $8.1 million as of September 30, 2026, compared to $7.7 million as of June 30, 2026, and $8.8 million as of December 31, 2025.

ABOUT DOLPHIN:

 

Dolphin (NASDAQ:DLPN) is where cultural creation meets marketing execution. Founded in 1996 by Bill O'Dowd, Dolphin operates as both a venture studio - developing and investing in breakthrough content, products and experiences - and a marketing consortium, featuring leading agencies across every communications discipline.

 

At its core, the venture studio creates, produces, finances, markets and promotes new businesses and cultural ideas - ranging from acclaimed film, television and digital content to consumer goods, live events and partnerships that define entertainment and lifestyle. Surrounding this entrepreneurial engine, Dolphin's marketing prowess brings together best-in-class firms including 42West, The Door, Shore Fire Media, Elle Communications, Special Projects and The Digital Dept. Together, this collective delivers unmatched cross-marketing expertise and relationships across every vertical of pop culture - from film, television, music, influencers, sports, hospitality and fashion to consumer brands and purpose-driven initiatives.  Use of Non-GAAP Financial Measures

 

In order to provide greater transparency regarding our operating performance, the financial results in this press release refer to non-GAAP financial measures that involve adjustments to GAAP results. Non-GAAP financial measures exclude certain income and/or expense items that management deems are not directly attributable to the Company’s core operating results and/or certain items that are inconsistent in amounts and frequency, making it difficult to perform a meaningful evaluation of our current or past operating performance.

 

Adjusted EPS is calculated by dividing adjusted EBITDA by the weighted average number of basic and diluted shares outstanding for periods in which the Company reports adjusted EBITDA, consistent with the Company’s convention for GAAP earnings per share. The Company uses the two-class method when calculating adjusted EPS in periods where the adjusted EBITDA results in income as the participating securities have the right to participate in undistributed earnings. To calculate the adjusted EPS, the adjusted EBITDA is reduced by the amount of the adjusted EBITDA that is available to the participating security holders.

 

Management believes that the presentation of operating results using this non-GAAP financial measure provides useful supplemental information for investors by providing them with the non-GAAP financial measure used by management for financial and operational decision making, planning and forecasting and in managing the business. This non-GAAP financial measure does not replace the presentation of financial information in accordance with U.S. GAAP. These non-GAAP financial results should not be considered a measure of liquidity and are unlikely to be comparable to non-GAAP financial measures provided by other companies.
