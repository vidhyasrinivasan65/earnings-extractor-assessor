Walnut Creek, CA – October 28, 2026 – (BUSINESS WIRE) – Mechanics Bancorp (Nasdaq: MCHB) (“Mechanics” or the “Company”), the financial holding company of Mechanics Bank, today announced its financial results for the quarter ended September 30, 2026. Mechanics reported net income of $46.0 million, or $0.20 per diluted share (3), for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $57.7 million, or $0.25 per diluted share, for the second quarter of 2026. For the nine months ended September 30, 2026, Mechanics reported net income of $147.8 million, or $0.64 per diluted share, compared to $154.6 million, or $0.72 per diluted share, for the nine months ended September 30, 2025.(4)
Third Quarter 2026 Highlights:
•Total assets of $20.9 billion at September 30, 2026, compared with $21.2 billion at June 30, 2026.
•Total loans of $13.0 billion at September 30, 2026, compared with $13.6 billion at June 30, 2026.
•Total deposits of $18.0 billion at September 30, 2026, compared with $18.1 billion at June 30, 2026, and noninterest-bearing deposits of $6.3 billion at September 30, 2026, compared with $6.4 billion at June 30, 2026.
•Loans-to-deposits ratio of 72% at September 30, 2026, compared with 75% at June 30, 2026.
•Total cost of deposits was 1.33% for the third quarter of 2026 and 1.25% for the second quarter of 2026.
•Noninterest expense was $114.2 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared with $124.5 million for the second quarter of 2026, reflecting lower acquisition and integration costs and salaries and employee benefits expense.
•Dividends paid in the third quarter of 2026 were $0.25 per share of Class A common stock and $2.50 per share of Class B common stock.
•Strong capital ratios (1), including an estimated 16.98% Total risk-based capital ratio, 14.73% Tier 1 capital ratio, 14.73% CET1 capital ratio and 8.74% Tier 1 leverage ratio at September 30, 2026.
•Allowance for credit losses (“ACL”) to total loans of 1.03%, down from 1.12% at the prior quarter-end.
•Total delinquent loans decreased to $67.5 million, from $95.4 million at June 30, 2026, representing 0.52% of total loans at September 30, 2026, compared to 0.70% at June 30, 2026.
•Completed the sale of $417 million of performing indirect auto loans, resulting in a $10.3 million loss on sale, as we further optimized the balance sheet by accelerating the exit from a non-core business line.
•Completed a $308 million securities portfolio restructuring, selling low-yielding AFS securities and reinvesting the proceeds in MBS at then current market rates, resulting in a $32.3 million loss on sale.
•Gain on other equity investments of $5.1 million for the third quarter of 2026 as a result of valuation updates.
•Non-recurring acquisition and integration costs of $0.6 million for the third quarter of 2026, compared to $5.9 million in the prior quarter.

Presentation of Results – HomeStreet Bank Merger
On September 2, 2025, the merger of HomeStreet Bank, the wholly owned subsidiary of Mechanics Bancorp (formerly known as HomeStreet, Inc.) with and into Mechanics Bank, was completed. Mechanics Bank is the accounting acquirer (legal acquiree), HomeStreet Bank is the accounting acquiree and Mechanics Bancorp is the legal acquirer. Mechanics’ financial results for all periods ended prior to September 2, 2025 reflect Mechanics Bank’s historical financial results on a standalone basis and results of the combined company beginning September 2, 2025. In addition, for periods prior to September 2, 2025, the number of shares issued and outstanding, earnings per share, and all references to share quantities or metrics of Mechanics have been retrospectively restated to reflect the equivalent number of shares issued in the merger since the merger was accounted for as a reverse acquisition. As the accounting acquirer, Mechanics Bank remeasured the identifiable assets acquired and liabilities assumed in the merger as of September 2, 2025 at their acquisition date fair values.
Adoption of Purchased Seasoned Loans Accounting Standard
The Company early adopted Accounting Standards Update (“ASU”) 2025-08, “Financial Instruments–Credit Losses (Topic 326): Purchased Loans,” during the fourth quarter of 2025. This new standard, which the Company elected to early adopt as of January 1, 2025, requires acquired loans that meet certain criteria at acquisition (purchased seasoned loans) to be recognized at their purchase price plus the amount of the allowance for expected credit losses (gross-up approach). As a result, for purchased seasoned loans acquired in the HomeStreet merger, the Company established an allowance for credit losses of $20.3 million at the date of acquisition for these loans and reversed the provision for credit losses recorded in the third quarter of 2025, and recorded it as part of the acquired loans initial amortized cost basis. Required disclosures regarding the impact of the adoption were presented when the Company filed its annual report on Form 10-K for the year ended December 31, 2025. In addition, third quarter 2025 results will be retrospectively adjusted when the Company files its quarterly report on Form 10-Q for the quarter ended September 30, 2026.
The impact of the adoption is reflected in the comparative prior period results presented in this earnings release for the quarter and nine months ended September 30, 2025 and as of September 30, 2025.

Net Interest Income
Third Quarter of 2026 vs. Second Quarter of 2026
Net interest income in the third quarter of 2026 was $2.3 million lower than the second quarter of 2026 primarily as a result of higher deposit costs and a $133.1 million decrease in average interest earning assets. Mechanics’ net interest margin decreased from 3.62% to 3.55% primarily due to higher deposit costs, partially offset by higher investment securities interest income from the partial quarter benefit of the AFS securities restructuring.
Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 vs. Nine Months Ended September 30, 2025
Net interest income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 increased $128.4 million as compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025 due primarily to an increase of $3.9 billion in average interest-earning assets, as well as an increase in net interest margin from 3.40% in the nine months ended September 30, 2025 to 3.59% in the nine months ended September 30, 2026, as a result of the HomeStreet merger.
Provision for Credit Losses
Third Quarter of 2026 vs. Second Quarter of 2026
The reversal of provision for credit losses in the third quarter of 2026, which consists of the provision for loans and unfunded commitments, was $15.2 million, compared to a reversal of provision of $2.8 million for the second

quarter of 2026. The reversal of provision for the third quarter was primarily driven by decreases in expected losses on our multifamily and CRE investor loans, corresponding decreases in qualitative factors and the auto loan portfolio sale.
Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 vs. Nine Months Ended September 30, 2025
The reversal of provision for credit losses was $10.2 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026, compared to a provision of $22.7 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2025. The decrease in provision for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 was driven primarily by the auto portfolio sale, lower qualitative adjustments, lower balances and lower unfunded commitments reserves.
Noninterest Income (Loss)
Third Quarter of 2026 vs. Second Quarter of 2026
Noninterest income (loss) in the third quarter of 2026 decreased $37.2 million from the second quarter of 2026 primarily due to the losses on the sales of auto loans and AFS securities during the quarter, partially offset by the increase in other noninterest income from a gain on other equity investments. In addition, noninterest income decreased in the third quarter of 2026 relative to the second quarter of 2026 due to the gain on sale of the Fannie Mae Multifamily Delegated Underwriting and Servicing (“DUS®”) business line in other noninterest income and a mortgage servicing rights valuation adjustment in net gain (loss) on sales of loans in the prior quarter.
Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 vs. Nine Months Ended September 30, 2025
Noninterest income for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 decreased $113.0 million from the nine months ended September 30, 2025 primarily due to the losses on the sales of auto loans and AFS securities during 2026 and the bargain purchase gain from the HomeStreet merger recognized in 2025. The decrease was partially offset by an increase in noninterest income that resulted from a gain on other equity investments and gain on sale of the DUS business line in other noninterest income and a mortgage servicing rights valuation adjustment in net gain (loss) on sales of loans in 2026.
Noninterest Expense
Third Quarter of 2026 vs. Second Quarter of 2026
Noninterest expense decreased $10.3 million in the third quarter of 2026 compared to the second quarter of 2026, primarily due to lower acquisition and integration related costs, and lower salaries and employee benefits expense due to headcount reductions as a result of continued integration following the HomeStreet merger.
Nine Months Ended September 30, 2026 vs. Nine Months Ended September 30, 2025
Noninterest expense increased $29.0 million for the nine months ended September 30, 2026 compared to the nine months ended September 30, 2025 primarily due to higher salaries and employee benefits expense, occupancy costs, equipment expense, and amortization of intangibles, partially offset by lower acquisition and integration related costs from the HomeStreet merger.
Income Taxes
Third Quarter of 2026 vs. Second Quarter of 2026
Our effective tax rate during the third quarter of 2026 was 26.4% as compared to 27.2% in the second quarter of 2026 and our federal statutory rate was 21.0%. The effective tax rate decreased compared to the prior quarter as a result of tax benefits related to stock awards that vested in the third quarter.

Investment Securities
Trading securities totaled $46.1 million and $46.6 million at September 30, 2026 and June 30, 2026. Securities available-for-sale increased by $173.0 million during the third quarter of 2026 to $4.3 billion at September 30, 2026, primarily due to purchases, partially offset by sales and paydowns during the quarter. The Company performed a restructuring of the balance sheet through the sale of low-yielding AFS securities and reinvestment in MBS at then current market rates near the end of July 2026. Securities held-to-maturity decreased by $27.3 million in the third quarter of 2026, primarily due to paydowns, and totaled $1.3 billion at September 30, 2026.
Loans
Total loans at September 30, 2026 were $13.0 billion, a decrease of $563.2 million from $13.6 billion at June 30, 2026, due primarily to the sale of auto loans and loan repayments during the quarter, partially offset by originations and advances.
Deposits
Total deposits decreased by $70.0 million during the third quarter of 2026 to $18.0 billion at September 30, 2026. The decrease was due to the runoff of $66.1 million of certificates of deposit and a $3.9 million decrease in core deposits.
Noninterest-bearing demand deposits totaled $6.3 billion and represented 35% of total deposits at September 30, 2026, compared to $6.4 billion, or 35% of total deposits, at June 30, 2026.
Borrowings
Total borrowings were zero at September 30, 2026, compared to $80.0 million at June 30, 2026. The decrease was due to payoff of short-term Federal Reserve Discount Window borrowings during the third quarter.
Equity
During the third quarter of 2026, total shareholders’ equity decreased by $122.4 million to $2.6 billion and tangible common equity (1) decreased by $115.5 million to $1.6 billion. The decrease in total shareholders’ equity for the third quarter of 2026 primarily resulted from a net decrease in retained earnings from net income, less dividends paid to common shareholders, and an increase in accumulated other comprehensive loss due to changes in fair value of securities available for sale from higher interest rates.
At September 30, 2026, book value per common share decreased to $11.59, compared to $12.15 at June 30, 2026. At September 30, 2026, tangible book value per common share (1) decreased to $7.05, compared to $7.56 at June 30, 2026.
