VIEMED HEALTHCARE ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER 2026 FINANCIAL RESULTS

Lafayette, Louisiana (November 4, 2026) Viemed Healthcare, Inc. (the “Company” or “Viemed”) (NASDAQ:VMD), a national provider of technology-enabled, home-based healthcare solutions and chronic disease management, announced today that it has reported its financial results for the three and nine months ended September 30, 2026, and updated guidance for the full year ending December 31, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Financial and Operational Highlights (all dollar amounts are USD):

•Net revenue for the quarter ended September 30, 2026 totaled a Company record $80.7 million, an increase of $8.8 million, or 12.2%, compared with the prior-year quarter, and 3.3% sequentially.

•Net income attributable to Viemed totaled $4.6 million, or $0.11 per diluted share, an increase of 32.0% compared to $3.5 million, or $0.09 per diluted share, in the prior-year quarter, and a 67.8% sequential increase from $2.8 million in the second quarter of 2026. Net income attributable to Viemed represented 5.7% of net revenue, compared to 3.5% in the preceding quarter.

•Adjusted EBITDA totaled $17.1 million, an increase of 5.8% year over year and 24.4% sequentially. Adjusted EBITDA as a percentage of net revenue increased to 21.1%, compared to 17.6% in the second quarter of 2026, representing an improvement of approximately 350 basis points.

•Ventilator rental revenue increased 7.4% year over year to $37.5 million, supported by higher average patient volumes. The Company's period-end ventilator patient count totaled 12,552 as of September 30, 2026.

•PAP therapy patients increased 37.5% year over year and 16.0% sequentially to 43,861 during the third quarter. Sleep resupply patients increased 22.1% year over year and 10.5% sequentially to 40,918.

•Net cash provided by operating activities totaled $11.5 million for the quarter and $53.9 million for the trailing twelve months ended September 30, 2026. Free cash flow totaled $4.0 million for the quarter and $26.1 million for the trailing twelve months ended September 30, 2026.

•The Company ended the quarter with $9.7 million in cash, $12.0 million in working capital, and approximately $46 million available under its existing credit facilities. During the quarter, the Company repaid $2.1 million of term debt and repurchased 115,000 common shares for approximately $1.0 million (excluding taxes).

Updated Full Year 2026 Guidance (all dollar amounts are USD):

Based on third quarter performance and increased visibility into the remainder of the year, the Company is raising the low end of its revenue guidance, narrowing its Adjusted EBITDA guidance range, and lowering its net capital expenditure outlook. The updated guidance reflects continued growth across the Company's diversified platform, anticipated sequential improvement in revenue and Adjusted EBITDA in the fourth quarter, and reduced capital intensity as less capital-intensive product and service offerings contribute a greater share of revenue.

•Net revenue is expected to be in the range of $316 million to $320 million, compared with the previous range of $314 million to $320 million.

•Adjusted EBITDA is expected to range from $64 million to $66 million, compared with the previous range of $64 million to $68 million.

•Net capital expenditures are now expected to range from 8.0% to 9.0% of net revenue, compared with the previous range of 8.5% to 10.0%.

This outlook excludes the impact of potential future acquisitions or other strategic transactions. See “Use of Non-GAAP Financial Information and Financial Guidance” below for further information about non-GAAP financial measures and non-GAAP financial guidance.




Casey Hoyt, Viemed’s CEO, commented:

“Viemed delivered another record quarter, with revenue reaching $80.7 million and continued growth across our respiratory, sleep, and women's health businesses. This marks our first full year-over-year quarterly comparison following the Lehan acquisition, and achieving double-digit revenue growth underscores the strength of our increasingly diversified platform and the opportunities ahead of us.”

“We are particularly pleased with the improvement in profitability this quarter. Our attributable net income increased 68% sequentially, while Adjusted EBITDA grew 24%, significantly outpacing revenue growth and demonstrating improved operating leverage. These results reflect the progress we anticipated following our investments earlier this year, and we see additional opportunities to improve efficiency as those initiatives mature. We remain focused on expanding our reach, delivering high-quality patient care, and building on this momentum as we finish the year.”

Reconciliation from GAAP Net Income to Non-GAAP Adjusted EBITDA

This press release refers to “Adjusted EBITDA”, which is a financial measure that is not prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles in the United States ("GAAP"). Adjusted EBITDA should be considered in addition to, not as a substitute for, or superior to, financial measures calculated in accordance with GAAP. Management believes Adjusted EBITDA provides helpful information with respect to the Company’s operating performance as viewed by management, including a view of the Company’s business that is not dependent on the impact of the Company’s capitalization structure and items that are not part of the Company’s day-to-day operations. Management uses Adjusted EBITDA (i) to compare the Company’s operating performance on a consistent basis, (ii) to calculate incentive compensation for the Company’s employees, (iii) for planning purposes, including the preparation of the Company’s internal annual operating budget, and (iv) to evaluate the performance and effectiveness of the Company’s operational strategies. Accordingly, management believes that Adjusted EBITDA provides useful information in understanding and evaluating the Company’s operating performance in the same manner as management. Adjusted EBITDA is not a measurement of the Company’s financial performance under GAAP and should not be considered as an alternative to revenue or net income, as applicable, or any other performance measures derived in accordance with GAAP. Adjusted EBITDA has limitations as an analytical tool and you should not consider it in isolation or as a substitute for analysis of the Company’s operating results as reported under GAAP. Adjusted EBITDA does not reflect the impact of certain cash charges resulting from matters the Company considers not to be indicative of ongoing operations; and other companies in the Company’s industry may calculate Adjusted EBITDA differently than we do, limiting its usefulness as a comparative measure. In calculating Adjusted EBITDA, certain items (mostly non-cash) are excluded from net income attributable to Viemed Healthcare, Inc., including depreciation and amortization of capitalized assets, net interest expense, stock based compensation, transaction costs, impairment of assets, and taxes.

Reconciliation from GAAP Net Cash Provided by Operating Activities to Non-GAAP Free Cash Flow

This press release refers to “free cash flow” which is a non-GAAP financial measure that does not have a standardized meaning prescribed by GAAP. Free cash flow is defined as net cash provided by operating activities less net capital expenditures (“Net CAPEX”). Net CAPEX is calculated as purchases of property and equipment minus proceeds from the sale of property and equipment. The Company's presentation of this financial measure may not be comparable to similarly titled measures used by other companies.

The Company presents free cash flow for the current quarter and trailing twelve months (TTM) as a supplemental liquidity measure. Management believes free cash flow provides investors with useful insight into the Company's ability to generate cash, fund growth initiatives, and return capital to shareholders. Free cash flow does not represent residual cash flow available for discretionary expenditures.
