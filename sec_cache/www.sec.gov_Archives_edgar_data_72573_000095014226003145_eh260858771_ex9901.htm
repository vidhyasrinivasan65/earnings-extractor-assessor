MOVADO GROUP, INC. ANNOUNCES THIRD QUARTER
FISCAL 2027 RESULTS

 

~ Net Sales of $198.3 million ~

~ Operating Income of $17.1 million ~

~ EPS of $0.56 ~

~ Board Declares Quarterly Dividend of $0.40 per share ~

 

Paramus, NJ – November 24, 2026 -- Movado Group, Inc. (NYSE: MOV) today announced third quarter and nine-month results for the periods ended October 31, 2026.

 

Third Quarter Fiscal 2027 Highlights

·Net sales increased 6.5% to $198.3 million versus $186.1 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026;
·Gross margin expanded 210 basis points to 56.4% compared to 54.3% in the third quarter of fiscal 2026;
·Operating income grew $5.4 million to $17.1 million, representing an increase of 46.2%, compared to $11.7 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 and increased 36.2% compared to adjusted operating income of $12.6 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026;
·Diluted earnings per share increased 33.3% to $0.56 compared to $0.42 in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 and increased 24.4% compared to adjusted diluted earnings per share of $0.45 in the third quarter of fiscal 2026; and
·The quarter ended with $207.2 million in cash and no debt.

 

Efraim Grinberg, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer, stated: “We delivered a strong third quarter, building on the momentum from the first half of the year. The quarter was highlighted by increases across our key financial metrics, including a 6.5% increase in net sales, with strength in our Movado brand and our licensed brands; a 210-basis-point expansion in gross margin; and a 33% increase in diluted earnings per share. These results underscore the strength of our business model, the power of our portfolio of global brands, and the excellent execution of our strategy by our management team, as we continue to focus heavily on the consumer—delivering high-impact innovation and enhanced customer engagement.”

 

“We ended the quarter with cash of $207.2 million and no debt, after investing to support our long-term growth and returning $25.6 million to shareholders through year-to-date dividend payments,” Mr. Grinberg continued. “As previously announced on October 8, 2026, we entered into an agreement to sell a 95% interest in our EBEL brand. This will allow us to concentrate on executing our priorities across our watch and jewelry portfolio, while placing EBEL with owners who have the expertise and commitment to support the next phase of growth for this exceptional Swiss brand.”

 

“While we continue to navigate a dynamic environment, we believe our disciplined execution of our strategic priorities, including continued innovation in our offerings and brand-building marketing initiatives, positions us well for the holiday season,” Mr. Grinberg stated further. “We remain confident in our ability to generate long-term profitable growth and increased value for our shareholders,” Mr. Grinberg concluded.

 

   

 

Non-GAAP Items (See attached table for GAAP and Non-GAAP measures)

 

Third quarter fiscal 2026 results of operations included a $0.9 million pre-tax charge, or $0.7 million after tax, representing $0.03 per diluted share, related to the investigation of misconduct within the Dubai branch of the Company’s Swiss subsidiary.

 

In this press release, references to “adjusted” results exclude the impact of the above charge and the impact of the items described in the Non-GAAP Items section of the Company’s earnings release for the first and second quarters of fiscal 2027 and 2026. Please refer to the attached GAAP and Non-GAAP measures table for a detailed reconciliation of the Company’s reported results to its adjusted, non-GAAP results.

 

Third Quarter Fiscal 2027 Results (See attached table for GAAP and Non-GAAP measures)

·Net sales increased 6.5% to $198.3 million, or increased 6.9% on a constant-dollar basis, compared to $186.1 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026. The increase in net sales was driven by growth across the Company’s owned and licensed brands and reflected approximately $5.0 million of customer shipments occurring earlier than originally anticipated. U.S. net sales increased 12.3%, as compared to the third quarter of fiscal 2026. International net sales increased 2.5% (3.1% on a constant-dollar basis), as compared to the third quarter of fiscal 2026.
·Gross profit was $111.8 million, or 56.4% of net sales, compared to $101.1 million, or 54.3% of net sales, in the third quarter of fiscal 2026. The 210 basis-point improvement in gross margin was primarily the result of positive channel and product mix, partially offset by higher fixed costs net of increased leveraging over higher sales, and higher shipping costs.
·Operating expenses were $94.6 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2027 compared to $89.3 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2026. This increase was primarily due to increased marketing expenses to support higher sales. As a percentage of net sales, operating expenses decreased to 47.7% of net sales from 48.0% in the prior-year period. Adjusted operating expenses in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 were $88.5 million, or 47.5% of net sales.
·Operating income was $17.1 million, or 8.6% of net sales, compared to $11.7 million, or 6.3% of net sales, in the third quarter of fiscal 2026. Adjusted operating income in the third quarter of fiscal 2026 was $12.6 million, or 6.8% of net sales.
·The Company recorded a tax provision of $5.4 million, or an effective tax rate of 29.8%, in the third quarter of fiscal 2027. The Company recorded a tax provision of $3.3 million and an adjusted tax provision of $3.5 million, or an adjusted tax rate of 25.2%, in the third quarter of fiscal 2026.
·Net income for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $12.9 million, or $0.56 per diluted share, compared to $9.6 million, or $0.42 per diluted share, in the third quarter of fiscal 2026. Adjusted net income was $10.2 million, or $0.45 per diluted share, in the third quarter of fiscal 2026.

 

Nine Months Fiscal 2027 Results (See attached table for GAAP and Non-GAAP measures)

·Net sales for the first nine months of fiscal 2027 increased 6.4% to $510.4 million, or increased 5.4% on a constant-dollar basis, compared to $479.7 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2026. The increase in net sales reflected increases across the Company’s owned and licensed brands and its Movado Company Stores. U.S. net sales increased 8.8%, as compared to the first nine months of last year. International net sales increased 4.7% (3.0% on a constant-dollar basis), as compared to the first nine months of last year.
·Gross profit was $294.2 million, or 57.6% of net sales, compared to $260.0 million, or 54.2% of net sales, in the first nine months of fiscal 2026. The 340-basis-point improvement in gross margin included $3.2 million, or 60 basis points, from IEEPA duty refunds, as well as the positive impact of changes in channel and product mix.
·Operating expenses were $255.2 million, compared to $243.9 million in the first nine months of fiscal 2026. As a percentage of sales, operating expenses were 50.0% of net sales versus 50.9% of net sales in the first nine months of last year. Adjusted operating expenses for the first nine months of fiscal 2027 were $254.5 million, or 49.9% of net sales, compared to $239.5 million, or 49.9% of net sales, for the first nine months of fiscal 2026. The increase in adjusted operating expenses was primarily due to higher performance-based compensation and increased marketing and selling expenses to support the increased sales.

·Operating income was $39.0 million, or 7.6% of net sales, compared to operating income of $16.0 million, or 3.3% of net sales, in the first nine months of fiscal 2026. Adjusted operating income was $39.7 million, or 7.8% of net sales, for the first nine months of fiscal 2027 compared to $20.5 million, or 4.3% of net sales, in the first nine months of fiscal 2026. Both operating income and adjusted operating income for the first nine months of fiscal 2027 included $3.2 million in IEEPA duty refunds.
·The Company recorded a tax provision of $10.9 million, or a tax rate of 25.3%, compared to a tax provision of $5.9 million, or a tax rate of 29.5%, in the first nine months of fiscal 2026. Based on adjusted pre-tax income, the adjusted tax provision for the first nine months of fiscal 2027 was $11.1 million, or an adjusted tax rate of 25.3%. This compares to an adjusted tax provision of $6.9 million, or an adjusted tax rate of 28.3%, in the first nine months of fiscal 2026.
·Net income was $32.1 million, or $1.40 per diluted share, compared to net income of $14.0 million, or $0.62 per diluted share, in the first nine months of last year. Adjusted net income for the first nine months of fiscal 2027 was $32.6 million, or $1.42 per diluted share, compared to adjusted net income of $17.4 million, or $0.77 per diluted share, in the first nine months of fiscal 2026. Both net income and adjusted net income for the first nine months of fiscal 2027 included $2.5 million in IEEPA duty refunds plus interest thereon, net of taxes.

 

 

   

 

Quarterly Dividend and Share Repurchase Program

The Company also announced that on November 24, 2026, the Board of Directors declared the payment on December 21, 2026, of a cash dividend in the amount of $0.40 for each share of the Company’s outstanding common stock and class A common stock held by shareholders of record as of the close of business on December 7, 2026.

 

During the first nine months of fiscal 2027, the Company repurchased 61,000 shares under its December 5, 2024, share repurchase program. As of October 31, 2026, the Company had $44.6 million remaining available under the share repurchase program.

 

EBEL

On October 8, 2026, the Company announced that it entered into a binding agreement to sell a 95% interest in its EBEL brand for $66.5 million, subject to customary purchase price adjustments. The closing is expected to take place before the Company’s fiscal year end.

 

Outlook

As previously noted, the Company has discontinued providing an annual outlook yet remains committed to providing transparency and insight into its business, markets, and current trends. Considering the early delivery of approximately $5.0 million of shipments in the third quarter as noted above, the Company expects fourth quarter sales to be approximately flat on constant-currency basis, with gross margin in the range of 55% to 56%, excluding any additional IEEPA duty refunds.

In this release, the Company presents certain financial measures that are not calculated according to generally accepted accounting principles in the United States (“GAAP”). Specifically, the Company is presenting adjusted operating expenses, adjusted operating income, adjusted pre-tax income, adjusted tax provision, adjusted tax rate, adjusted net income and adjusted diluted earnings per share, which are operating expenses, operating income, pre-tax income, tax provision, net income and diluted earnings per share, respectively, under GAAP, adjusted to eliminate costs due to the investigation referred to above and the establishment of a provision for a cost-savings initiative. The Company believes the adjusted measures are useful because they give investors information about the Company’s financial performance without the effect of certain items that the Company believes are not characteristic of its usual operations. Additionally, the Company is presenting constant-currency information to provide a framework to assess how its business performed excluding the effects of foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations in the current period. Comparisons of financial results on a constant-dollar basis are calculated by translating each foreign currency at the same U.S. dollar exchange rate as in effect for the prior-year period for both periods being compared. The Company believes this information is useful to investors to facilitate comparisons of operating results. These non-GAAP financial measures are designed to complement the GAAP financial information presented in this release. The non-GAAP financial measures presented should not be considered in isolation from or as a substitute for the comparable GAAP financial measures, and the methods of their calculation may differ substantially from similarly titled measures used by other companies.
