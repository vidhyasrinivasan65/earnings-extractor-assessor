Resideo Announces Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results and Updates 2026 Outlook
•Total revenue of $727 million, up 3% year-over-year, and revenue excluding OEM Security up 6% year-over-year
•Gross margin of 40.4%, marking the 14th consecutive quarter of year-over-year gross margin expansion
•GAAP income from continuing operations, net of taxes, of $31 million, down 66% compared to $90 million in third quarter of 2025; Standalone Adjusted EBITDA (1)of $154 million, up 5% year-over-year
•GAAP diluted EPS from continuing operations of $0.14, down 71% year-over-year; Standalone Adjusted diluted EPS (1) of $0.52, up 8% year-over-year
•Repaid $1.1 billion of gross debt; total gross debt of $2.1 billion at end of third quarter

SCOTTSDALE, Ariz., November 10, 2026 – Resideo Technologies, Inc. (NYSE: REZI), a leading global developer and manufacturer of critical control and sensing solutions for residential markets, today announced financial results for the third fiscal quarter ended October 3, 2026.
Management Remarks
“Resideo delivered top and bottom line third quarter results that were near the high end of our outlook ranges in our first quarter following the separation of ADI, underscoring our team’s continued strong execution. We achieved 3% revenue growth year-over-year across all sales channels and 6% growth year-over-year excluding OEM Security, driven by customer demand for our differentiated, non-discretionary products. We also reduced our debt by $1.1 billion, using the $900 million dividend received from ADI and $200 million of cash on hand,” said Tom Surran, Resideo's President and CEO.

“We remain focused on creating value for our customers with our differentiated products and services. That includes new AI-powered capabilities on our next-generation FORTIQ software platform, which delivers actionable intelligence that enhances customer productivity and optimizes the end-user’s experience. We are executing on our strategic priorities with discipline and consistency, creating positive momentum in a soft housing market and a dynamic macro environment.”

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights

Total revenue of $727 million was up 3% compared to $703 million in the third quarter of 2025, and includes an unfavorable impact of approximately 50 basis points from foreign currency exchange. Total revenue was near the high-end of the provided outlook range for the third quarter of 2026.

Revenue excluding OEM Security of $694 million, was up 6% compared to $653 million in the third quarter of 2025, and grew year-over-year across all of our sales channels and product categories due to volume, driven by customer demand for our products, and pricing. OEM Security revenue of $33 million was down 34% compared to $50 million in the third quarter of 2025, and declined as expected due to reduced volumes from a large OEM Security customer.

Gross margin of 40.4%, marking the 14th consecutive quarter of year-over-year gross margin expansion. Gains from price increases, operational efficiencies, and tariff refunds were partially offset by unfavorable product sales mix and inflationary input costs.

Research and development expenses increased $4 million from the third quarter of 2025 driven by investments in new product launches to drive future growth. Selling, general and administrative expenses increased $24 million from the third quarter of 2025, driven primarily by $30 million of non-recurring business separation expenses associated with the ADI

GAAP income from continuing operations, net of taxes, of $31 million was down 66% compared to $90 million in the third quarter 2025. Standalone Adjusted EBITDA(1) of $154 million was up 5% compared to $146 million in the third quarter of 2025, primarily due to higher gross profit dollars and controlled growth in operating expenses. Standalone Adjusted EBITDA was near the high-end of the provided outlook range for the third quarter of 2026.

Cash Flow and Liquidity
Net cash provided by operating activities of continuing operations was $33 million in the third quarter 2026 compared to net cash used by operating activities of continuing operations of $1.59 billion in the third quarter of 2025. The increase in cash provided by operations was primarily due to the $1.59 billion payment made in the third quarter of 2025 to Honeywell to terminate the Indemnification Agreement.

In connection with the ADI Spin-Off, Resideo received a $900 million cash dividend from ADI as partial consideration for the assets transferred in the ADI Spin-Off. We used this dividend, along with $200 million of cash on hand to repay $1.1 billion of gross debt during the third quarter. We also made a $20 million payment to ADI as part of the post-separation adjustment mechanism to true up cash. At October 3, 2026, Resideo had cash and cash equivalents of $229 million and total outstanding gross debt of $2.1 billion.

In connection with the completion of the ADI Spin-Off, Resideo’s outstanding Series A Cumulative Convertible Participating Preferred Stock (“Preferred Stock”) was reduced by 150,000 shares, leaving 350,000 shares outstanding, with a proportional adjustment to the conversion price and an ongoing smaller quarterly dividend payable on Resideo’s Preferred Stock.

ADI Spin-Off and Discontinued Operations
On August 3, 2026, Resideo completed the separation of its former ADI Global Distribution segment by distributing to Resideo common shareholders on a pro rata basis all of the issued and outstanding common stock of ADI Global Distribution Inc., the entity incorporated to hold the ADI business (the “ADI Spin-Off”). As a result of the ADI Spin-Off, we present the historical results of the ADI business as discontinued operations in our financial statements.

About Resideo

Resideo is a global building technologies company that is a leading developer and manufacturer of critical control and sensing solutions for residential markets. The company serves professional installers and integrators across diverse product categories, such as heating, ventilation, and air conditioning controls, combustion, life safety, security, and water. Its comfort and protection solutions can be found in more than 150 million residential and commercial spaces globally, with tens of millions of new devices sold annually. More information about Resideo and its trusted brands, including BRK, First Alert, and Honeywell Home, is available at resideo.com.
