KinderCare Reports Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Third Quarter Highlighted by Actions to Enhance Center Operations and Continued Strength in Champions.

Company Updates Full-Year Outlook.

Lake Oswego, Ore. (November 12, 2026) – KinderCare Learning Companies, Inc. (NYSE: KLC) (“KinderCare,” the “Company,” and “we”), a leading provider of high-quality early childhood education, today announced financial results for the third quarter ended October 3, 2026.

Third Quarter 2026 Highlights

•
Revenue of $665.3 million
•
Loss from operations of $75.5 million
•
Net loss of $85.0 million and net loss per common share, diluted of $0.71

Non-GAAP financial measures

•
Adjusted EBITDA (1) of $42.5 million
•
Adjusted net loss (1) of $3.0 million and adjusted net loss per common share, diluted (1) of $0.03

“While third-quarter enrollment at KinderCare Learning Centers fell short of our expectations, recent improvements reinforce our confidence in the operational changes we've made,” said Tom Wyatt, Chairman and Chief Executive Officer of KinderCare Learning Companies. “We’ve strengthened field leadership and helped center teams focus on the critical work of welcoming and supporting families. Since the end of back-to-school, new enrollments have outpaced the same period last year, although overall enrollment remains below last year’s levels. Additionally, Champions’ strong results, progress at Crème and opportunities in KinderCare for Employers give us a foundation to build on heading into 2027.”

Mr. Wyatt continued, “We know families face tough financial choices, and we’re focused on demonstrating the value of KinderCare’s high-quality education and care across all of our brands. Throughout it all, our focus remains unwavering: to reach more children and families, continue investing in our people and classrooms, and make a lasting difference in communities nationwide."

Third Quarter 2026 Financial Results

Total revenue decreased $11.5 million, or 1.7%, to $665.3 million for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to $676.8 million for the third quarter of 2025.

Revenue from early childhood education centers decreased by $19.6 million, or 3.1%, for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the third quarter of 2025. The decrease was driven from 5.9% lower enrollment, partially offset by 2.8% increase from higher tuition rates. The impact of operating fewer centers from our ongoing center optimization initiative reduced enrollment by approximately 190 basis points for the three months ended October 3, 2026 as compared to the three months ended September 27, 2025.

Revenue from before- and after-school sites increased by $8.2 million, or 16.4%, for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to the third quarter of 2025 primarily due to increased enrollment and opening new sites.

Loss from operations was $75.5 million for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to income of $26.3 million for the third quarter of 2025, a decline of $101.8 million. The decrease was driven by an $83.4 million increase in impairment losses in the third quarter of 2026 primarily due to the deterioration in our market capitalization from the decline in our stock price in the period triggering goodwill impairment, more centers closed and centers with lower operational performance resulting in higher impairment of long-lived assets, as well as indefinite-lived intangible asset impairment related to certain legacy trade names and trademarks. During the third quarter of 2026, the Company closed 20 early childhood education centers as part of an on-going center optimization initiative. Additionally, the decrease was attributable to an increase in cost of services of $8.1 million, primarily due to higher rent, insurance, janitorial, utilities and food costs, combined with an increase in marketing spend which was partially offset by lower personnel costs due to reduced labor hours as a result of lower enrollment. Selling, general and administrative expenses remained relatively consistent for the third quarter of 2026 compared to the third quarter of 2025, due to reduced expenses from long-term compensation plans offset by increased severance expense incurred from organizational changes during the quarter as well as higher closed center costs due to our center optimization initiative.

Net loss was $85.0 million for the third quarter of 2026 as compared to net income of $4.6 million for the third quarter of 2025, a decline of $89.6 million. The decrease was primarily driven by the loss from operations noted above, partially offset by a $9.9 million decrease in income taxes, resulting from an income tax benefit in the third quarter of 2026 compared to income tax expense in the comparative period. Net loss per common share, diluted was $0.71 for the third quarter of 2026 compared to net income per common share, diluted of $0.04 for the third quarter of 2025.

For the third quarter of 2026, adjusted EBITDA (1) decreased $23.9 million, or 35.9%, to $42.5 million, and adjusted net income (1) decreased $18.3 million, to an adjusted net loss (1) of $3.0 million, from the third quarter of 2025. Adjusted net loss per common share, diluted (1) was $0.03 for the third quarter of 2026 compared to adjusted net income per share, diluted (1) of $0.13 for the third quarter of 2025.

As of October 3, 2026, the Company operated 1,556 early childhood education centers and 1,208 before- and after-school sites.

Balance Sheet and Liquidity

As of October 3, 2026, the Company had $143.4 million of cash and cash equivalents and $187.7 million of available borrowing capacity under the revolving credit facility, after giving effect to the outstanding letters of credit of $74.8 million.

During the nine months ended October 3, 2026, the Company generated $109.8 million in cash provided by operating activities and made net investments totaling $91.3 million, primarily from purchases of property and equipment. Additionally, during the nine months ended October 3, 2026, the Company utilized $8.4 million in cash for financing activities.

2026 Outlook

Based on current trends and outlook, the Company is updating its guidance ranges for the full year 2026. Revenue is now expected to be approximately $2.64 billion to $2.66 billion and adjusted EBITDA is expected to be approximately $190 million to $195 million (2). Adjusted net income per common share, diluted is expected to be approximately $0.02 to $0.04 (2). The Company will provide additional details on its outlook during its earnings conference call.

About KinderCare Learning Companies™

KinderCare Learning Companies, Inc. (NYSE: KLC) is a leading private provider of early childhood and school-age education and care, KinderCare builds confidence for life in children and families from all backgrounds. KinderCare supports hardworking families in 42 states and the District of Columbia with differentiated flexible child care solutions:

•
In neighborhoods, with KinderCare® Learning Centers that offer early learning programs for children six weeks to 12 years old;
•
Crème School®, which offers a premium early education experience using a variety of enrichment classrooms; and
•
In local schools, with Champions® before and after-school programs.

KinderCare partners with employers nationwide to address the child care needs of today’s dynamic workforce. We provide customized family care benefits for organizations, including care for young children on or near the site where their parents work, tuition benefits, and backup care where KinderCare programs are located. Headquartered in Lake Oswego, Oregon, KinderCare operates more than 2,700 early learning centers and sites.

Explanation of add backs:

 

(1)
Represents impairment charges for goodwill, indefinite-lived intangible assets, and long-lived assets. Goodwill impairment recognized during the three and nine months ended October 3, 2026 of $71.4 million and $344.9 million, respectively, was driven by the further deterioration in our market capitalization from a continued decline in our stock price during the first and third quarters of fiscal 2026. Impairments of long-lived assets for the periods presented was a result of reduced operating performance at certain centers due to the impact of changing demographics in certain locations in which we operate and current macroeconomic conditions on our overall operations, as well as centers closed or identified for closure and early lease terminations executed. During the three and nine months ended October 3, 2026 the Company recognized indefinite-lived intangible assets impairment of $3.7 million as the result of fully impairing certain legacy trade names and trademarks.
(2)
Represents non-cash stock based compensation expense in accordance with Accounting Standards Codification (“ASC”) 718, Compensation: Stock Compensation.
(3)
Includes expense reimbursements and revenue arising from the COVID-19 pandemic, net of pass-through expenses incurred as a result of certain grant requirements. During the nine months ended September 27, 2025, we recognized $30.1 million of Employee Retention Credits ("ERC") offsetting cost of services (excluding depreciation and impairment) as well as $2.1 million in professional fees in selling, general, and administrative expenses as a result of calculating and filing for ERC. COVID-19 Related Stimulus is net of pass-through expenses incurred as stipulated within certain grants of $1.9 million during the nine months ended September 27, 2025. Additionally, we recognized $0.7 million during the nine months ended September 27, 2025 in funding for reimbursement of center operating expenses in cost of services (excluding depreciation and impairment).
(4)
For both the three and nine months ended September 27, 2025, other costs includes $0.7 million in transaction costs associated with repricing our First Lien Term Loan Facility and for the nine months ended September 27, 2025, other costs also includes $0.2 million in costs related to our IPO. These costs represent items management believes are not indicative of core operating performance.
(5)
Includes the tax effect of the non-GAAP adjustments, calculated using the appropriate federal and state statutory tax rate and the applicable tax treatment for each adjustment. The non-GAAP tax rate was 1.7% and 40.8% for the three and nine months ended October 3, 2026, respectively, and 25.8% for both the three and nine months ended September 27, 2025. Our statutory rate is re-evaluated at least annually.
