SunPower Q3’26 Financial Recovery
Q/Q: Revenue Up $10.2M, Losses Reduced By 3.6x

(herein “SunPower,” the “Company,” or Nasdaq: “SPWR”), a solar technology, services, and installation company, will present its Q3’26 results via webcast today, Thursday, November 19, 2026, at 1:00pm ET. Our Q3’26 revenue was $65.1 million, up $10.2 million from $54.9 million in the prior quarter. Our non-GAAP Q3’26 operating loss narrowed to $4.9 million, a 3.6x reduction from the $17.8 million in Q3’26, which contained a write-off of aged, uncollectable AR, some of it dating all the way back to “old” SunPower. The Q3’26 GAAP income statement includes a new line item (footnote 4) to book a one-time, non-cash charge of $22.6 million for a second group of aged, uncollectable backlog that – after one additional year of validation – has also been judged to be uncollectable (company out of business, etc.). This new charge has been offset with reserves of the same amount in the opex line. The reserve for this $22.6 million charge to our Q3’26 GAAP results has been excluded from our non-GAAP results because it is a one-time, non-cash event. Finally, our Q3’26 ending cash balance was $13.1 million.

Q4’26 Revenue Outlook
Solar industry forecasts for a given Q4 are usually 20% or more below the Q3 actuals, due to winter and for this Q4, the solar Income Tax Credit (ITC) cancellation. Nevertheless, we are targeting a Q4’26 revenue of $61.8 million, five percentage points below our Q3’26 performance of $65.1 million.
Conclusion
Rodgers concluded, “After two poor quarters, Q1’26 (our first 10K audit line-item cleanup) and Q2’26 (uncollectible AR cleanup), we made structural changes across SunPower and achieved a meaningful recovery in both revenue and profit in Q3’26. Since this report has focused on those problems and solutions, I have chronicled our significant technical accomplishments during Q3’26 in an Appendix.”

Appendix: Events of Note this Quarter
SunPower Introduces the Monolith II Solar Panel
Next-Generation Premium High-Wattage Panel, Exclusive to SunPower
Maximizes Customer Value with More Kilowatt Hours Per Year
The Monolith II panel leverages Singapore’s Renewable Energy Corporation’s (REC’s) advanced N-Type Heterojunction (HJT) cell technology – which provides superior power output and annual energy production, exceptional aesthetics and a 25-year warranty. The new 522-watt “bifacial” panel has glass on both the front and back sides to illuminate the cells from both sides. More information can be found here.

SPWR Completes First Commercial Monolith Installation at Fortinet Headquarters
SunPower announced the completion of its first commercial installation of Monolith panels for the headquarters of Fortinet, the global cybersecurity provider in Sunnyvale, California. The Monolith panel provided the customer with the same wattage as its prior system, but with 40% less rooftop area. More information can be found here.
SunPower Expands in Texas with SOLRITE Virtual Power Plant (VPP) Partnership
SunPower announced an expansion in Texas through the acquisition of prime project assets from Houston installer Greenlight Solar, as well as a strategic partnership with Fort Worth financier SOLRITE Energy to expand its residential solar and storage offerings and accelerate its Virtual Power Plant (VPP) business. More information can be found here.
SunPower Breaks Ground on Reno AI Data Center
SunPower broke ground in October on the first phase of the Reno AI data center project. This is another new-revenue market for SunPower. Additional information can be found here.

SunPower Completes Megawatt Solar Project
Awarded High Customer NPS Score on Millenium Project
A Megawatt of Power Produced from Carport Roofs
SunPower completed the Millenium Project, a megawatt solar system constructed on architectural carports in Palm Desert, California, which provides power to a 330-housing unit community. This project will generate over 1.6 million kWh of clean, locally produced energy annually and was awarded a Net Promoter Score (NPS) of 90 by the customer, a significant milestone in SunPower’s ongoing customer transformation program. More information can be found here.
