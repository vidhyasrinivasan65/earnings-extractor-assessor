Titan Machinery Inc. Announces Results for Fiscal Third Quarter Ended October 31, 2026
- Gross Margin Expanded 250 bps y/y as Inventory Actions Continue to Drive Margin Recovery -
- Updates Fiscal 2027 Segment Modeling Assumptions; Reaffirms Profitability Outlook -
West Fargo, ND – November 24, 2026 – Titan Machinery Inc. (Nasdaq: TITN) ("Titan" or the "Company"), a leading network of full-service agricultural and construction equipment stores, today reported financial results for the fiscal third quarter ended October 31, 2026.
“Our fiscal 2027 third quarter results reflect the progress we have made on inventory health, which is translating to meaningful improvement in equipment margins and helped us deliver a profitable quarter despite lower revenue,” stated Bryan Knutson, Titan Machinery’s President and Chief Executive Officer. “Importantly, those gains are coming from the work our team has done to significantly improve the quality and mix of the inventory we carry while reducing interest bearing floorplan levels. Demand across our domestic Agriculture business played out largely as we anticipated, and our Construction and Australia segments both delivered growth, while conditions in Europe remained soft. Our parts and service businesses continue to provide an important foundation, and our team remains focused on the areas within our control as we work through the bottom of this cycle.”
Fiscal 2027 Third Quarter Results
Consolidated Results
For the third quarter of fiscal 2027, revenue was $545.7 million compared to $644.5 million in the third quarter last year. Equipment revenue was $361.3 million for the third quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to $459.9 million in the third quarter last year. Parts revenue was $121.3 million for the third quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to $122.3 million in the third quarter last year. Service revenue was $46.7 million for the third quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to $48.9 million in the third quarter last year. Rental and other revenue was $16.4 million for the third quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to $13.3 million in the third quarter last year.
Gross profit for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $107.4 million, compared to $111.0 million in the third quarter last year. Gross profit margin was 19.7% in the third quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to 17.2% in the third quarter last year. The year-over-year improvement in gross profit margin primarily reflects stronger equipment margins given continued reductions in aged inventory, alongside a higher mix of parts and service revenue.
Operating expenses were $100.6 million for the third quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to $100.5 million in the third quarter last year. Operating expenses as a percentage of revenue were 18.4% for the third quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to 15.6% of revenue in the third quarter last year.
Floorplan interest expense and other interest expense decreased to $8.2 million in the third quarter of fiscal 2027, compared to $10.9 million for the same period last year. The decrease was driven by lower interest-bearing inventory levels.
In the third quarter of fiscal 2027, net income was $1.2 million, with earnings per diluted share of $0.05, matching the results from the prior year period.
Adjusted EBITDA in the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $15.4 million, compared to $17.2 million in the third quarter last year.

Segment Results
Agriculture Segment - Revenue for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $358.7 million, compared to $420.9 million in the third quarter last year, reflecting a same-store sales decrease of 13.1%. The decrease resulted from softer demand for equipment compared to the prior year period, driven by continued pressure on grower profitability. Pre-tax income for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $5.9 million, compared to $6.1 million in the third quarter last year.
Construction Segment - Revenue for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $79.5 million, compared to $76.7 million in the third quarter last year, reflecting a same-store sales increase of 3.6%, which was primarily due to higher new equipment sales and rental revenue. Pre-tax loss for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 improved to $0.5 million, compared to $1.7 million in the third quarter last year.
Europe Segment - Revenue for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $61.0 million, including a $0.8 million benefit related to foreign currency fluctuations versus the prior year period, compared to $117.0 million in the third quarter last year. Net of the effect of these foreign currency fluctuations, revenue decreased $56.8 million, or 48.5%. The wind-down of the Company's German operations contributed approximately $12 million of the year-over-year revenue decrease in the third quarter of fiscal 2027. The remainder of the decrease was primarily due to lower equipment demand compared to the prior year period, as the prior period had been driven by stronger sales resulting from European Union stimulus programs in Romania. Demand in Ukraine also softened during the quarter due to grain marketing logistics challenges in the region. Pre-tax results for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 were approximately break-even, compared to pre-tax income of $3.5 million in the third quarter last year.
Australia Segment - Revenue for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $46.5 million, including a $3.5 million benefit related to foreign currency fluctuations versus the prior year period, compared to $29.9 million in the third quarter last year. Net of the effect of these foreign currency fluctuations, revenue increased $13.1 million, or 43.8%. The increase was primarily due to higher equipment sales, as healthy rainfall and the resulting prospect for strong yields across the Company’s footprint improved customer sentiment. Pre-tax loss for the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $1.1 million, compared to $3.8 million in the third quarter last year.
Balance Sheet and Cash Flow
Cash at the end of the third quarter of fiscal 2027 was $25.3 million. Total inventories decreased by $30.4 million to $872.7 million as of third quarter end, as compared to January 31, 2026. Equipment inventories decreased by $38.7 million to $686.5 million as of third quarter end, as compared to January 31, 2026. Outstanding floorplan payables were $549.6 million on $1.5 billion total available floorplan and working capital lines of credit as of October 31, 2026, compared to $553.8 million of outstanding floorplan payables as of January 31, 2026.
For the nine months ended October 31, 2026, the Company's net cash provided by operating activities was $13.6 million, compared to $83.9 million for the nine months ended October 31, 2025. The decrease in cash from operating activities was primarily attributable to a smaller reduction in inventories compared to the prior year period and changing mix in floorplan financing, which was partially offset by lower receivable and other working capital outflows.
Additional Management Commentary
Mr. Knutson continued, “Current farm economics continues to impact equipment purchasing decisions, as the late-summer lift in commodity prices was short-lived. Against that backdrop, we are reiterating our fiscal 2027 modeling assumptions for profitability measures, while updating our segment revenue assumptions to reflect our year-to-date performance. We are raising our outlook for Agriculture, reflecting our year-to-date performance and expectations for the fourth quarter, as well as for Australia, where healthy rainfall and the prospect for strong yields have improved customer sentiment. In Europe, we are narrowing our outlook

The synergies of this joint venture should benefit our construction business once the transaction closes."

About Titan Machinery Inc.
Titan Machinery Inc., founded in 1980 and headquartered in West Fargo, North Dakota, owns and operates a network of full service agricultural and construction equipment dealer locations in North America, Europe and Australia, servicing farmers, ranchers and commercial applicators. The network consists of US locations in Colorado, Idaho, Iowa, Minnesota, Nebraska, North Dakota, South Dakota, Wisconsin and Wyoming. The international network includes European stores located in Bulgaria, Romania, and Ukraine and Australian stores located in New South Wales, South Australia, and Victoria in Southeastern Australia. Our stores offer one or more of the CNH Industrial Brands, including Case IH, New Holland Agriculture, Case Construction, New Holland Construction, and CNH Industrial Capital. Additional information about Titan Machinery Inc. can be found at www.titanmachinery.com.
