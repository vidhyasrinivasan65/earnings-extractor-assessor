Gladstone Capital Reports
Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Results
Net Investment Income of $0.49 Per Share for the Fourth Quarter and $2.00 Per Share for Fiscal 2026
Net Asset Value of $20.77 Per Share
Conference Call on Wednesday, November 18, 2026, at 8:30 a.m. ET
McLean, VA, November 17, 2026: Gladstone Capital Corporation (Nasdaq: GLAD) (the “Company”) today announced earnings for its fourth quarter and fiscal year ended September 30, 2026. Please read the Company’s Annual Report on Form 10-K filed today with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (the “SEC”), which is available on the SEC’s website at www.sec.gov and the Investors section of the Company’s website at www.GladstoneCapital.com.
Fourth Quarter and Fiscal Year 2026 Highlights:
• | Net Investment Income: $11.0 million, or $0.49 per share, for the quarter, and $45.1 million, or $2.00 per share, for fiscal 2026
• | Distribution Coverage: Net investment income after preferred stock dividends covered 100% of the regular monthly distributions for the quarter and 104.5% for fiscal 2026
• | Net Asset Value: $20.77 per share; the $0.73 decrease during the quarter reflects $0.55 per share of net realized and unrealized losses, and the $0.18 per share supplemental distribution
• | Distributions: Paid regular monthly distributions of $0.45 per common share and a supplemental distribution of $0.18 per common share during the quarter
• | Investment Activity: Invested $83.9 million during the quarter and $308.7 million during fiscal 2026, including $171.4 million in 11 new portfolio companies
• | Non-Accruals: Loans to the same five portfolio companies as the prior quarter remained on non-accrual status, representing 2.8% of debt investments at fair value after markdowns
• | Leverage: Total principal debt of $447.5 million; debt-to-equity of 1.08x including the Series A preferred stock as debt, compared to 1.00x at June 30, 2026
Comments from Gladstone Capital’s Chief Executive Officer, Bob Marcotte:
“Gladstone Capital closed fiscal 2026 with a $956 million investment portfolio, an 11.7% increase in total investment income and $2.00 in net investment income per share. Our longstanding focus on the lower middle market has built strong sponsor relationships and a steady flow of attractive lending opportunities. We originate and underwrite our own investments, and the same team remains actively involved through exit. Fourth-quarter unrealized depreciation was concentrated in two existing non-earning investments and several previously appreciated equity positions. We know these businesses well and are managing each situation with the same discipline that has served us well over the past 25 years. Our priority for fiscal 2027 is to translate portfolio growth into higher earnings and deliver strong economic returns for our shareholders.”

Fourth Fiscal Quarter 2026 Results:
Investment Income: Total investment income was $24.6 million, up 0.3% from the prior quarter, as the weighted average principal balance of interest-bearing investments and the weighted average yield were largely unchanged.
Net Expenses: Total expenses, net of credits, were $13.6 million, up 0.5%, as higher interest expense and base management fees more than offset a $0.6 million decrease in the net incentive fee, which included a $0.5 million credit from the Adviser.
Net Investment Income: Net investment income was $11.0 million, or $0.49 per share. After $0.9 million of preferred stock dividends, it covered approximately 100% of the $10.2 million of regular monthly distributions declared, excluding the $0.18 per share supplemental distribution.
Net Assets: The net decrease in net assets resulting from operations applicable to common stockholders was $2.2 million, or $0.10 per share, driven by $13.6 million of net unrealized depreciation, including $9.2 million on control investments. Net asset value per share decreased $0.73, or 3.4%, to $20.77, reflecting $0.55 per share of net realized and unrealized losses and the $0.18 per share supplemental distribution paid to stockholders during the quarter.
Fiscal Year 2026 Results:
Investment Income: Total investment income increased 11.7% to $99.6 million. Interest income increased 9.7% to $95.8 million, as the weighted average principal balance of interest-bearing investments grew 17.9%, partially offset by a decline in the weighted average yield to 11.9% from 12.7%. Other income increased $1.9 million to $3.8 million on higher dividend income.
Net Expenses: Total expenses, net of credits, increased 24.0% to $54.4 million, reflecting a $5.0 million increase in interest expense on higher average borrowings under the Credit Facility, a $2.9 million increase in the net base management fee on higher average total assets and a $1.9 million increase in the net incentive fee, which included $0.5 million of credits from the Adviser compared to $2.3 million in the prior year.

Net Investment Income: Net investment income was $45.1 million, or $2.00 per share, as higher expenses offset the growth in investment income. After $2.6 million of preferred stock dividends, it covered 104.5% of the $40.7 million of regular monthly distributions declared.
Net Assets: The net increase in net assets resulting from operations applicable to common stockholders was $32.0 million, or $1.42 per share, as $20.2 million of unrealized depreciation on control investments more than offset net gains on other investments. Net asset value per share decreased $0.57, or 2.7%, to $20.77.
Portfolio and Investment Activity:
As of September 30, 2026, the Company’s portfolio had a fair value of $955.8 million, with a cost basis of $984.8 million, in 59 portfolio companies across 17 industries. The portfolio at fair value was comprised of 72.9% secured first lien debt, 15.8% secured second lien debt, 4.7% preferred equity and 6.5% common equity and equivalents. For the quarter ended September 30, 2026, the Company invested $83.9 million, including $32.8 million in two new portfolio companies and $51.1 million in existing portfolio companies, and received $70.7 million of repayments and net proceeds. For fiscal 2026, the Company invested $308.7 million, including $171.4 million in 11 new portfolio companies and $137.4 million in existing portfolio companies, and received $210.0 million of repayments and net proceeds.
As of September 30, 2026, loans to five portfolio companies were on non-accrual status with a cost basis of $52.9 million, or 6.0% of the cost basis of all debt investments in the portfolio, and a fair value of $23.8 million, or 2.8% of the fair value of all debt investments in the portfolio, compared to $50.5 million, or 5.8%, and $30.6 million, or 3.6%, for the same five portfolio companies as of June 30, 2026. The lower fair value reflects markdowns during the quarter. No investments were added to or removed from non-accrual status during the quarter.
Liquidity and Capital Resources:
As of September 30, 2026, the Company had cash and cash equivalents of $1.8 million and $188.0 million outstanding under its $385.0 million revolving credit facility, with approximately $177.7 million available under the borrowing base, subject to covenants and other restrictions, and $259.5 million in aggregate principal amount of notes outstanding, for total principal debt of $447.5 million. During the quarter, the Company increased commitments under its revolving credit facility by $20.0 million to $385.0 million.
Debt-to-equity, including the Series A preferred stock at its liquidation preference as debt, was 1.08x on net assets of $469.3 million at September 30, 2026, compared to 1.00x at June 30, 2026 (0.95x and 0.90x on principal debt alone). The Company’s asset coverage on senior securities representing indebtedness was 212.7%, compared to the 150% minimum required under the Investment Company Act of 1940. Floating-rate borrowings represented 42.0% of principal debt, and the next scheduled note maturity is $50.0 million of 3.75% Notes due May 1, 2027. During the quarter, the Company issued $6.8 million of its 6.25% Series A Cumulative Redeemable Preferred Stock through its continuous offering; 2,283,854 preferred shares were outstanding at September 30, 2026 with an aggregate liquidation preference of $57.1 million.
